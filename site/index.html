Look closer

150,000 particles.

Each one has its own spring, its own timing and its own path through a turbulence field. All of them are simulated, projected and lit in WebAssembly, on the main thread, in a few milliseconds per frame.

Move your cursor through them. Click to send a shockwave.

Performance

Native speed where it counts.

The math runs in WebAssembly: timing, easing, springs, physics, projection and depth sorting. JavaScript only touches what changed. Every figure below comes from scripts in the repo.

Plain object property writes cost the same in every JS library. Anikit's wins come from moving heavy work into WebAssembly and skipping unchanged DOM writes. Full methodology is in the README.
