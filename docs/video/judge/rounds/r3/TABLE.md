Top gaps per brief:

- **a-social**: It needs an idea specific to this coffee (origin, process, ritual) rather than a cup, word swap and packshot · Typography with a point of view: scale contrast, layout and interaction with the liquid or steam instead of centred captions · Real photographed or lit material and camera movement that link the frames, instead of four static centred slides · Motion is mostly hard swaps between static posters. A top studio would make liquid, fruit and pack physically turn into one another, and here nothing moves enough to carry the 'pour' idea
- **c-event**: It needs a concept: make the 'substation' electricity actually drive the typography (current running through the stencil gaps, letters flickering on in sequence, voltage distortions) · Break the one-layout structure so each frame is a different poster with scale contrast, cropping and full-bleed type, as 'TICKETS FRIDAY' only begins to attempt · Add material and light, such as industrial grit, scan noise and real glow from the bolt lighting the letterforms, so the dark ground stops reading as an empty UI · Motion has no physical language. The bolt should drive everything: names struck or energised in, rows charging along a circuit, a discharge as the release. Instead it is cuts and scale bumps on a beat grid, with no spacing curves, overlap or weight.
- **e-data**: There is no world-building or change of scale. A's move from nautilus to nebula shows how to give the data a physical, textured subject (real Arctic geography, light, ice material) · The layout should change across beats, with real choreography (camera moves, type interacting with the ice) instead of swapping numbers in a fixed template · Typography needs a point of view, for example custom numerals or type that cracks or melts with the subject, rather than a default serif-and-mono pairing · Motion: one locked 30 s frame where the only moving things are a counter, rings and bars, so roughly half the seconds look like the previous one. There's no camera move, change of scale or reframing to mark the decades or the 2012 record. Top studios would push in on 2012, pull out for the −36% reveal, and give the ring deposits some weight and settle so each year lands as an event rather than a step in an interpolation.
- **h-ident**: It needs a specific idea beyond 'bird flies'. For example, the hover-and-stoop behaviour of a kestrel could drive the edit and the type, the way A's fall drives its narrative. · Composition and camera never vary. It needs scale shifts, cropping, off-centre framing and real cuts. · Typography, light and texture are defaults. It needs a typographic point of view and real atmospheric light or material, like B's metalwork or D's print texture. · Motion: there is no hover physics or choreography. Top studios would animate the kestrel's signature (the locked head, the working wings and tail, and the gust-and-correct rhythm) and build tension to a stoop or a wing-snap that drives the reveal.
