# Nike ~ Air Max (Air Max '17, "The Art of Negative Space") — ManvsMachine (2016)

**Why it's in the set:** the reference for a pure-CG, product-as-metaphor sneaker film: 32 s of rapid visual metaphors, each a single designed still-life, tied together by one idea (air = negative space) and a footstep-built soundtrack; Cannes Gold Lion for Design Craft.

## The idea
"Go Lighter, Go Longer." The film is "a metaphorical exploration of air and the negative space it occupies": the Air Max 2017's lightness and cushioning are shown through visual metaphors drawn from an everyday run (*source*: studio's Vimeo description). Stash reports the team deliberately overloaded the viewer with metaphors in three families: the product, the urban running environment, and pop culture, with the shoe's negative space (the visible Air unit) present in every shot as the connective thread (*source*: Stash).

## Structure and pacing
- Length 32 s (*source*: oEmbed).
- A rapid-fire sequence of separate, highly detailed vignettes; the creative director told Stash that shots were "very different" from one another and each received more detail than usual (*source*: Stash). No published shot count; *est.* 15–20 shots, median ≈ 1.5–2 s (from the 32 s length and the "rapid-fire" description), so pace sits at the fast/steady boundary.
- Each vignette is a centred, single-object composition on a seamless coloured ground (thumbnail, and the four press stills reproduced by It's Nice That: pale-blue cables knotting on a matching blue ground; white/light-blue/black fabric ribbons on grey; a sneaker built from soft grey pebbles on red; the Swoosh broken into five white segments on grey).

## Typography
Almost none. The only type in the press frames is the **AIRMAX** wordmark (Nike's italic, wide, outlined Air Max logotype) printed on a clear Air unit (thumbnail). Type is part of the object (a print on a material), not an overlay.

## Colour and light
- Pastel, one-colour seamless grounds per shot: powder/teal blue (≈ #9FCAD6 *est.* from thumbnail), mid grey, signal red (It's Nice That captions).
- Soft, large-source studio light, very low contrast, gentle contact shadows; the ground colour bleeds into the object (blue tint on the white knit tubes in the thumbnail). No pure blacks or blown whites.

## Motion techniques
- Soft-body / inflatable objects: in the thumbnail the shoe upper is a stack of nine knit tubes (perforated mesh texture, shading from white to blue) arching over a clear, gas-filled Air bag; tubes knot and tangle in another shot (It's Nice That captions).
- Objects deform, squash, segment and reassemble (Swoosh in five pieces; shoe made of pebbles); motion is light, bouncy and "whimsical" (*source*: Stash quoting the CD).
- Physically believable materials at macro scale: knit fabric with visible pores, clear TPU with refraction and rim highlights.

## Transitions
Hard cuts between self-contained vignettes (*est.*, from the vignette structure); continuity comes from the recurring negative-space motif rather than from match-cuts.

## Sound design and music
Sound-design led, by Zelig: footsteps were recorded, then edited and re-pitched to form the melody and rhythm; the bed includes foot strikes, percussive traffic noises, airflow and the runner's breath (*source*: Stash). The rhythm of the edit is the rhythm of a run.

## What makes it world-class
1. One idea carries every shot: each vignette is a different metaphor, yet every one shows "air"/negative space, so 15+ unrelated images read as one film.
2. Material truth at macro scale: knit pores, clear-plastic refraction, soft contact shadows; at a 100% crop the objects look tangible, not "CG primitive".
3. Restraint in composition: one object, one colour ground, centred, generous margins; the shot reads in under a second.
4. The score is made of the subject (footsteps, breath, traffic) and the cuts follow it.
5. Playful physics (squash, knotting, bouncing) gives weight without realism.

## What Anikit would need
- `scene3d` with a soft-key studio setup, seamless coloured cyc, DOF off or very shallow, `motionBlur`; `post` "clean".
- Audio: `audio` cues with custom footstep samples cut to the edit; beat markers for cuts.
- GAP: soft-body/inflatable tube simulation and a knit/perforated fabric material.
- GAP: clear refractive material (air bag) with believable caustics/rim light.

## Judge's checklist
- Does every shot express the same single idea (a recurring motif present in each frame)?
- Is each frame one object on one flat, deliberate colour ground, readable in < 1 s?
- At 100% crop, do materials show real surface detail (pores, refraction, contact shadow) rather than smooth primitives?
- Is the soundtrack built from sounds of the subject, with cuts landing on those hits?
- Is the light soft and low-contrast with no crushed blacks or clipped whites?
