# XREAL 1S AR Glasses TVC — Morph Studio (2025)

**Why it's in the set:** a looping 30 s-class TV spot that tells a product's colour and tech story as biology (embryo → cell division → birth), entirely in one signature blue; a recent Asia-Pacific benchmark for fluid/organic CG wrapped around a hard-surface product.

## The idea
Morph "emphasized the 1S's distinctive blue aesthetic, employing the concept of biological evolution": the glasses are "an organism gestating in an embryo," going from gene expression and cell division to full development and "breaking free." The narrative is cyclical so it can loop, the model continuously "self-evolving through decomposition and reorganization" (*source*: Morph via Stash).

## Structure and pacing
- 26 s upload (*source*: oEmbed); Stash calls it a looping 30-second TV spot.
- Stages (*source*): gene expression → cell division → development → breaking free → (loop).
- *est.* 12–16 shots, median ≈ 1.5–2 s (from 26 s and a five-stage arc).

## Typography
End/hero frame (thumbnail): **XREAL** wordmark in white, heavy geometric sans caps with a soft glow, left of the product; **1S** in lighter-weight white sans to the right; both on the product's horizontal centre line, flanking it. Type is small relative to the product (≈ 10% frame height *est.*).

## Colour and light
One hue family: ultramarine/cobalt blue (≈ #1A3CC8 to #0A1440 *est.* from thumbnail) over navy-black with fine star-like specks. The product (lavender-grey frames, dark lenses with reflections) sits above a swirling liquid surface of marbled blue/white with glossy blue droplets/spheres around it. Light is cool and glossy; specular highlights on the droplets and lens reflections do the work.

## Motion techniques
- Houdini FX (four FX artists credited) for liquid, droplets and cell-like division; After Effects for finishing (*source*: toolkit).
- Bionic/organic form designed for the product; decomposition and reassembly of the model (*source*).
- Seamless loop structure (*source*).

## Transitions
Morph-based: the product decomposes and reorganises between stages (*source*); *est.* continuous rather than graphic transitions.

## Sound design and music
Music/sound design by VNTA (*source*); not described further.

## What makes it world-class
1. Concept, colour and brand are one thing: the product's signature blue is the medium of the whole film.
2. Organic simulation (fluid, droplets, cell division) contrasted with the precise hard-surface product.
3. Loopable narrative: the last frame can return to the first without a visible seam.
4. The hero frame is a classic lock-up: wordmark and model name on the product's centre line, product dominant.
5. Glossy specular detail (droplets, lens reflections) holds at full resolution.

## What Anikit would need
- `scene3d` with glossy/clear materials, `glow`, `camera` DOF, `motionBlur`; `text` lock-up with a soft glow; `post` cool grade.
- Loop: matching first/last state (expressions or `loop`).
- GAP: liquid/FLIP-style simulation with droplets interacting with a product.
- GAP: organic subdivision/cell-division morph of a mesh.

## Judge's checklist
- Is the whole film in one brand hue family, used as the concept rather than decoration?
- Is there a clear contrast between organic simulation and the hard-surface product?
- Does the last frame loop cleanly to the first?
- Is the end lock-up (wordmark + model name) aligned to the product's centre line and crisp?
- Do highlights on liquid and glass stay clean, without banding or noise?
