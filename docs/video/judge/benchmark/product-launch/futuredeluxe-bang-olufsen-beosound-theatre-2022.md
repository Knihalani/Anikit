# Bang & Olufsen - Beosound Theatre — FutureDeluxe (2022)

**Why it's in the set:** a 30 s luxury-hardware reveal in one continuous journey (eye → fabric world → product) where the camera move *is* the transition, and the product is withheld until the last beat; benchmark for "premium" CG lighting and cloth.

## The idea
Built on B&O's line "A New Vision for Sound": the emotional connection between person and product, through vision and sound. The film opens on a photorealistic (CG) human eye, travels *through* it into a cascading fabric world that teases the soundbar's features and finishes, then the fabric sweeps away to reveal the whole product, which is itself clad in Kvadrat fabric (*source*: studio Vimeo description; Stash).

## Structure and pacing
- 30 s (*source*: oEmbed). Three acts: eye (macro) → fabric journey (feature teases) → reveal (*source*: description).
- *est.* 8–12 shots, median ≈ 2.5–3 s (from the three-act description and "builds anticipation"), many linked by continuous camera travel, so it reads as fewer, longer moves than the shot count suggests.

## Typography
None documented in-film; the opening thumbnail is type-free. Any end card is not described; treat type as absent/minimal.

## Colour and light
- Opening frame (thumbnail): letterboxed (≈ 2.4:1 image inside the 16:9 upload), extreme close-up of an ice-blue iris against warm bronze skin with gold-glitter eyeshadow; strong warm/cool split: cool key on the iris (catchlight upper left), warm practical-like light on the right with a specular streak on the sclera; deep falloff to black at frame left.
- Grade by Miguel Wratten (*source*: credits): rich blacks, warm metallic highlights (bronze/gold echoes the product's metal finishes).

## Motion techniques
- Camera push into the pupil as the transition into the next world (*source*: "we journey through it").
- Large-scale fabric simulation (Houdini/VFX artist credited) cascading and finally sweeping away to unveil the product (*source*).
- Macro detail: skin pores, glitter particles, iris fibres visible in the thumbnail at 1280 px.

## Transitions
In-camera: through-the-eye push and a fabric "wipe" reveal (cloth sweeping off the product). No graphic transitions documented.

## Sound design and music
"Stirring electro-string soundtrack" that builds anticipation toward the reveal (*source*: Stash); sound design by Zelig Sound.

## What makes it world-class
1. A single continuous journey with in-camera transitions (into the eye, through cloth) rather than cuts between unrelated shots.
2. The product is withheld until the final beat; every earlier shot teases a material or finish, so the reveal pays off.
3. Photoreal macro: iris fibres, skin texture and glitter hold at 100% crop; the eye is CG and is indistinguishable from live action in the still.
4. Motivated, warm/cool split lighting with real falloff to black (no flat fill).
5. Materials rhyme: B&O fabric (Kvadrat) and metal finishes are previewed by the cloth world and the bronze skin tones.

## What Anikit would need
- `scene3d` with physically based materials, shallow `camera` DOF, `motionBlur`, `post` "cinematic" with letterbox, a camera fly-through spanning scenes (transition via camera rather than `transitions`).
- `audio` with a licensed string score and sfx swells.
- GAP: photoreal human eye/skin rendering (subsurface scattering, glitter).
- GAP: large cloth simulation that collides with and slides off a product model.

## Judge's checklist
- Is the product withheld until the last 20–25% of the runtime, with earlier shots teasing its materials?
- Are transitions done in-camera (push-through, cloth wipe) instead of crossfades?
- At 100% crop, does the macro opener show real micro-detail (pores, fibres, sparkle)?
- Is the light split warm/cool with deep, clean blacks and a clear key?
- Does the music build continuously to a peak that coincides with the reveal?
