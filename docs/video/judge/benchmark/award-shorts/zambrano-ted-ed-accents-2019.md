# "Accents" by Denice Frohman — Robertino Zambrano (2019)

**A spoken-word poem animated frame by frame, the drawing scratched into ink and driven by the poet's rhythm — the Annecy commissioned-film Cristal of 2019.**

## The idea
TED-Ed's *There's a Poem for That* series pairs poems with animators; Denice Frohman's "Accents" is about her mother's Puerto Rican accent and language as identity. Zambrano turned the performance's energy into "vibrant and raw frame-by-frame animation", imagery "scratched into an inky distance", with the lyrics driving the images (*source*: Stash).

## Structure and pacing
- 2 min 33 s (*source*: Annecy listing). Fast, poem-driven: images change on phrases, *est.* median 1–2 s with continuous metamorphosis between ideas.

## Typography
*observed* (Stash still): words drawn by hand as glyph-like runes — "BONE" with a diamond for O, strokes scratched in salmon-red (~#F07060) with a soft glow, letters built from straight angular lines. Type is part of the drawing, appearing on key words of the poem.

## Colour and light
*observed*: near-black ground with deep maroon mottling (~#1A0608 → #3A0E12), lines in hot salmon/red, dust and scratch speckle across the frame. "A grain and patina" (*source*: LBB quoted on Nexus Studios). Light is emissive: the lines glow against darkness.

## Motion techniques
Frame-by-frame 2D animation by seven animators (*source*: credits); line boil from redrawing; metamorphosis of one image into the next; texture overlays (*est.* from "grain and patina").

## Transitions
Morphs between drawings rather than cuts; flashes on stressed syllables (*est.*).

## Sound design and music
Frohman's own performance as narration; music and sound design by Bamm Bamm Wolfgang (*source*). VO-led, with percussion locking images to her cadence (*est.*).

## What makes it world-class
1. The animation is timed to a human voice's rhythm, image changes on stressed syllables.
2. Hand-made texture everywhere (scratch, grain, boil): no frame looks digital-clean.
3. Typography drawn as part of the image, on the words that matter.
4. A limited palette (black, maroon, salmon-red) with emissive line light.

## What Anikit would need
`text` with per-glyph custom paths or hand-drawn `image` sequences, `shape` strokes with `wigglePath` for boil, `glow`, `post` grain/scratches, `audio` VO with beat/word markers for sync. GAP: true frame-by-frame drawn animation and etched-line texture.

## Judge's checklist
- Do image changes land on the stressed words/syllables of the narration?
- Is there hand texture (grain, boil, scratch) consistently across every frame?
- Is type integrated into the image style rather than set as captions?
- Does the limited palette hold for the whole piece?
