# PICTOPLASMA OPENER 2023 — Will Anderson (2023)

**A one-minute character performance, made by one artist in Blender in five days, that beat studio productions to Annecy's commissioned-film Cristal.**

## The idea
Pictoplasma (the Berlin character-design festival) asked Will Anderson for "a kind of meditation"; he combined it with his cat, Greg, and the idea of a character coming into existence, ending with the world collapsing in on itself (*source*: Stash). A frazzled cat on a seamless studio cyc stares at the viewer while its world misbehaves.

## Structure and pacing
- 1 min 12 s (*source*: Annecy listing). Essentially one character in one space; *est.* 3–8 shots, mostly a locked or slowly creeping camera. Calm surface, comic tension underneath.

## Typography
None in the stills (*observed*); festival branding at end (*est.*).

## Colour and light
*observed* (Stash still 2, YouTube hq2): near-monochrome — mid-grey fur with white guard hairs, dark charcoal backdrop wings and a white cyc floor; the only colour is the cat's acid yellow-olive eyes (~#C9B21C) and pink inner ears. Soft top/front key, crisp specular in the eyes, strands catching light at the silhouette. Thin white electric squiggles crackle from the fur (static) — *observed*.

## Motion techniques
Blender, mostly motion capture for face and body, made in five days (*source*: Stash). Hair dynamics: fur that frizzes, stands on end and settles; deadpan micro-performance (eye darts, blinks) (*est.* from stills and the "frazzled" description).

## Transitions
*est.*: one continuous scene ending in a collapse of the set/world rather than cuts.

## Sound design and music
Music by Martin Green; sound design and mix by Keith Duncan (*source*). Sound carries the comedy (purrs, static crackle, silence) — *est.*

## What makes it world-class
1. A single, specific character with a readable inner state (frazzled) at thumbnail size.
2. Material richness in near-monochrome: thousands of hair strands with rim light, colour saved for the eyes.
3. Restraint: one set, one subject, one joke, executed perfectly in 72 s.
4. Authorship: a clear personal voice instead of a generic "3D showcase".

## What Anikit would need
`scene3d` with a character `model`, a cyc environment, `camera` slow push, `post` grain, `audio` sound design. GAP: hair/fur groom and dynamics; mocap-driven rig; static-electricity line FX (could use `shape` paths with `wigglePath`).

## Judge's checklist
- Does one subject hold the frame with a clear emotional state?
- Is colour withheld so a single accent (here, the eyes) owns attention?
- At 100% crop, do edges and fine detail (hair, strands) hold without aliasing or shimmer?
- Is the comedy/tension carried by timing and sound, not by cuts?
