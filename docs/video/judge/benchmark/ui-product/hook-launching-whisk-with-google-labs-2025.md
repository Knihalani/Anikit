# Launching Whisk with Google Labs — Hook (2025)

**Why it's in the set:** turns what "could have been a very dry how-to explainer" into "a music-driven flurry of motion" (*source*: Stash): a UI demo of an AI image tool told with kinetic type, one flat brand yellow, cursor-driven picks and a payoff image. The reference for showing an AI product's input → output loop.

## The idea
Whisk lets you provide separate image references for **subject**, **scene** and **style** and combines them. Hook's film "finds a balance between demonstrating the product and tapping into the joy of the creative process"; they prompted and collected many example images "to shuffle through at every step", and showed uploading your own images and "rolling the dice" to randomise (*source*: Hook via Stash). Hook's case study: "we used kinetic type, bold colors, gradients, and custom sfx with music to explain the process" (*source*: byhook.com).

## Structure and pacing
- 52 s (*source*: YouTube listing). Hook says it was briefed as a 20 s spot and "had to triple the length" to include all features (*source*: Stash). Stash post May 2025; Hook's YouTube upload 3 Oct 2025 (*source*).
- 25 % (hq1): flat yellow ground, label "SUBJECT" in small tracked caps above a dashed-outline image slot holding a photo of an older man in a straw hat and teal jacket; a large arrow cursor clicks it; a small yellow dice/badge at top right of the slot; neighbouring slots are white blanks entering from the sides (carousel).
- 50 % (hq2): three slots in a row "SUBJECT / SCENE / STYLE", each ticked with a yellow check badge: man photo, a mossy overgrown ruin, an anime-style red-haired portrait; a dice icon top right.
- 75 % (hq3) and thumbnail: the generated result full-frame (the man, now anime-styled, reading a book while riding a green flying craft through a vine-covered city) with the end line "START / **whisking** / TODAY" in yellow over it; the end card already holds by 75 %.
- *est.* 25–35 shots/beats, median ≈ 1–1.5 s.

## Typography
- Lowercase "whisking" in a large geometric sans (Google Sans–like *est.*), yellow #F5DC00-ish (*est.*), with small tracked uppercase "START" / "TODAY" above/below: a stacked lock-up centred over the image.
- Category labels ("SUBJECT", "SCENE", "STYLE") in small bold tracked caps, black on yellow.

## Colour and light
One flat saturated yellow ground for all UI steps; black labels; white placeholder slots; colour otherwise comes from the images themselves. The result image gets a slight darkening so yellow type reads on it.

## Motion techniques
- Cursor-driven selection: big arrow cursor clicks a slot; images shuffle through a slot (randomise) before landing (*source*: "examples to shuffle through at every step").
- Carousel/row layouts sliding horizontally; check badges pop on selection.
- Kinetic type for feature names (*source*: Hook).

## Transitions
Hard cuts and slides on beat between yellow UI steps; a final reveal of the full-bleed generated image (*est.* from frames).

## Sound design and music
Music-driven with custom sfx (*source*: Hook).

## What makes it world-class
1. The product's mental model (subject + scene + style = result) is the structure of the film; a viewer learns the tool by watching.
2. One flat brand colour holds every UI step together; images supply all other colour.
3. The payoff is a genuinely surprising output image, held full-bleed with the call to action.
4. A cursor with clear, large clicks and per-click sfx gives rhythm to a demo.
5. Labels are tiny and tracked, so the images stay the hero.

## What Anikit would need
- `composition` scenes on a flat `background`; `image` layers in rounded slots with dashed `stroke`; `component` "cursor" clicks; rapid image swaps (expressions or `animate` on `src` via multiple layers with `start`); `text` kinetic for "whisking"; `transitions` `slide`/`cut`; `audio` click/pop sfx on `@beat`.
- No major GAP; quality depends on the example images.

## Judge's checklist
- Does the film's structure mirror the product's input → output model?
- Is one flat brand colour used for every UI step, with images providing other colour?
- Does every click have a visible cursor press and an audible sfx on the beat?
- Is there a payoff output held full-bleed long enough to enjoy (≥ 2 s)?
- Are labels small and tracked so content dominates?
