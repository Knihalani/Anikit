# Introducing the new Notion AI — Notion (2024)

**Why it's in the set:** an 8 M-view (*source*: YouTube listing at time of research) feature launch that mixes real product UI, bold kinetic headlines and Notion's line-drawn AI face mascot on a light ground, using graphic wipes between colour fields; the reference for a *light-mode*, witty SaaS film.

## The idea
"Meet the new Notion AI—the helpful assistant you didn't know you need": it can search Notion, Slack and Google Drive, generate and edit content, analyse PDFs and images, and chat using real-world knowledge (*source*: YouTube description). The film personifies the assistant as Notion's minimal line-drawn face in a circle and shows each capability as a UI moment.

## Structure and pacing
- 57 s (*source*: YouTube listing); published 25 Sep 2024 (*source*: YouTube).
- 25 % (hq1): three-panel split: left a white chat panel (UI), centre a saturated blue (#1B7FE3 *est.*) block with the AI face in a white circle, right an off-white panel with a cropped headline "…ers / …apps" and the Google Sheets icon. Panels move horizontally (mid-wipe).
- 50 % (hq2): Notion AI chat answer panel (bulleted status summary with @-mentions, "13 sources found", Copy button, "Ask AI anything…" input) over a blurred dashboard with bar and line charts, beside the AI face on blue.
- 75 % (hq3): small AI face centred on off-white (#F4F4F3 *est.*) inside letterbox bars: a calm hold/end beat.
- *est.* 20–25 shots, median ≈ 1.5–2 s; capability → UI proof → next capability.

## Typography
- Headlines in a heavy neo-grotesk (Notion's Inter-based brand sans, *est.*), black on off-white, tight tracking, two-line left-aligned stack ("Meet the *new* / Notion AI").
- Emphasis by switching one word to italic and underlining it with a hand-drawn brush stroke (thumbnail: "new").
- UI text at product size inside the chat panel.

## Colour and light
Off-white #F7F7F5-ish ground, black type and line art, one saturated blue field as the accent block; product icons (Google Sheets green, Slack) bring the only other colour. Flat, no lighting; thin drop shadows under UI cards.

## Motion techniques
- Horizontal panel wipes / push between colour fields and UI (hq1 caught mid-transition).
- UI cards slide in over blurred real dashboards (depth via blur, not 3D).
- Mascot face as a recurring anchor; line-art hand-drawn accent marks.

## Transitions
Graphic panel wipes and pushes (hq1), hard cuts on beat (*est.*).

## Sound design and music
Not documented; *est.* light, rhythmic track with UI sfx.

## What makes it world-class
1. A brand character (the line-drawn AI face) gives a software feature a personality without a mascot animation budget.
2. Every capability is proven by a real UI moment (chat answer with sources, connected-app icons).
3. One accent colour field (blue) structures the frame; everything else is black on off-white.
4. Hand-made marks (brush underline) on a precise grotesk add wit.
5. Graphic panel wipes keep the film moving without 3D or camera tricks.

## What Anikit would need
- `composition`/`box` for UI panels with real text; `text` with inline italic span; `shape` `path` with `trimEnd` for the underline draw-on; `transitions` `push`/`wipe`; `camera` small pans; background blur on a layer (`blur`); `audio`.
- GAP: brush-textured stroke for hand-drawn marks.

## Judge's checklist
- Is there exactly one accent colour block, with everything else black/off-white?
- Does each claimed capability get a real UI proof shot with specific content?
- Is emphasis typographic (italic + hand-drawn underline) rather than effects (glow, gradient)?
- Are transitions graphic (wipes/pushes between panels) and cut on the beat?
- Does a recurring brand device (character/logo) anchor the film at start and end?
