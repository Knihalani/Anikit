# The AIRMAX 1000 #shorts — Nike (2024)

**Why it's in the set:** a vertical product short that frames a tall, printing sneaker in a 3D-printer gantry, using the 9:16 frame's height for the machine-and-object composition; reference for CG product macro in vertical.

## The idea
The Air Max 1000 is a 3D-printed reimagining of the Air Max 1, made with Zellerfeld and unveiled at ComplexCon Las Vegas in Nov 2024 (*source*: designboom / search summaries). The CG launch visuals told "a story behind the making of the Nike Airmax 1000 and its sustainability", based on Zellerfeld's production process, and were the main visuals for the launch and the ComplexCon booth (*source*: Already Been Chewed via Stash, Feb 2025). Upload date of this Short not verified; year = product launch.

## Structure and pacing
- 9:16 confirmed (*source*: oEmbed 113×200).
- oardefault still: the shoe (laceless red knit-textured upper with a visible Air window and Swoosh relief) stands toe-up on a ribbed grey support base on a black print bed, the print head (white housing, fan, cables) touching the top of the shoe; frame-filling vertical gantry; soft warm out-of-focus factory windows behind.
- hq1 (25 %): darker close-up: print head above a partially printed red shoe (lugged outsole visible) on a white support, cool grey shadows.
- hq3 (75 %): extreme macro of red mesh with a hot white-orange glow (extruder/heat) and bokeh particles.
- *est.* 10–15 shots, median ≈ 1–1.5 s.

## Typography
None in frames seen (platform title carries text).

## Colour and light
One hero colour (Nike University Red-like, #C8102E *est.*) against neutrals (bone white, warm grey, black). Soft window light from behind (warm, blown slightly), a top key on the print head, and in macros a practical heat glow as the motivated light.

## Motion techniques
CG macro with shallow DOF; print head moving over the form; heat glow and floating particles in macro; *est.* growth/extrusion reveal of the shoe.

## Transitions
*est.* hard cuts between wide (machine) and macro (material) shots.

## Sound design and music
Not documented.

## What makes it world-class
1. The vertical frame is used for a tall subject (shoe toe-up + gantry), so nothing is cropped or letterboxed.
2. One saturated hero colour against neutrals.
3. Process as story: the manufacturing method (printing) is the spectacle.
4. Motivated light (window back-light, extruder glow).
5. Macro texture (knit/print ridges) holds at full resolution.

## What Anikit would need
- `scene3d` with product mesh, area back-light, emissive glow, shallow `camera` aperture, `motionBlur`; `component` "sparks" or particles for macro; `post` warm grade.
- GAP: layer-by-layer growth reveal of a mesh; photoreal machine set.

## Judge's checklist
- Is the subject composed vertically (tall axis used), with no letterbox or wasted side space?
- Is there one hero colour against neutrals?
- Is the light motivated by the set (windows, emissive parts)?
- Does the process (how it's made) drive the shots?
- Do macro shots show surface detail without noise or banding?
