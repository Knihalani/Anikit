# Instagram — Motion Identity System (sizzle) — Studio Dumbar/DEPT® (2024)

**Why it's in the set:** the award-winning motion system behind Instagram's brand, i.e. the motion grammar that Instagram's own vertical marketing is built from. The upload is a 16:9 sizzle (aspect verified via oEmbed: 426×240), so it is the one non-vertical piece in this category; it is here for the motion language, not the frame shape.

## The idea
Instagram refreshed and expanded its identity (brand identity by 2x4, custom typeface by Colophon) and asked Studio Dumbar/DEPT® for "a vibrant, all-encompassing motion system" (*source*: studio page / ED Awards). Motion "echoes the imperfection of both natural human movement and user-made content"; some techniques "mimic natural camera movement (suggesting the depth and shifting perspectives of 3D space), while others recall the action of swiping or scrolling"; "defining the system's physics … played a fundamental role"; content and creators always come first (*source*). Guidelines, templates and toolkits were built for designers worldwide; campaigns premiered on two big screens over Penn Plaza (*source*).

## Structure and pacing
- 38 s sizzle (*source*: oEmbed), uploaded 11 Mar 2024 (*source*).
- *est.* 25–35 shots, median ≈ 1 s: a showcase of the motion techniques applied to type, UI and creator content.

## Typography
Instagram Sans (custom typeface by Colophon, *source*) in white on the brand gradient; the wordmark seen in the thumbnail is the rounded, humanist Instagram Sans with its distinctive looped "g".

## Colour and light
Thumbnail: the Instagram gradient (orange #FF8A00 → magenta/pink #FF0069 → purple #7638FA, *est.*) as a soft field with the white wordmark centred. Here the gradient is the brand asset itself, not a decorative blob (contrast with BAR.md #1/#2: the gradient is earned because it *is* the logo colour).

## Motion techniques
Camera-like moves with depth and shifting perspective; swipe/scroll-derived moves; deliberately imperfect, human-feeling physics (*source*). Applied to brand elements, type and creator content (*source*).

## Transitions
Swipe/scroll-style moves as transitions (*source*: "recall the action of swiping or scrolling").

## Sound design and music
Studio Dumbar lists sound design among its services on the project (*source*: studio page); details not published.

## What makes it world-class
1. Motion is a system with named physics, derived from how the app is used (swipe, scroll, camera), not a one-off animation.
2. Content and creators come first; brand motion frames user content rather than replacing it.
3. Built to scale: toolkits/templates let other designers produce consistent work worldwide.
4. Movement is deliberately "imperfect" (human), avoiding sterile linear easing.
5. Typography (custom Instagram Sans) and gradient are used as the brand's own assets.

## What Anikit would need
- `text` with a custom font asset, `mask` reveals, `camera` moves with `depth` parallax, swipe-like `transitions` (`push`/`slide`) with custom eases, `motionBlur`.
- GAP: a reusable motion-token system (named spring/ease presets per technique) that specs can reference.

## Judge's checklist
- Do transitions borrow gestures from the platform (swipe, scroll) rather than generic fades?
- Does movement have a consistent, named physics (same overshoot/settle language throughout)?
- Is user/creator content the hero, with brand motion framing it?
- Is any gradient a brand asset used deliberately, not a background filler?
- Is type set in a deliberate (custom) face and held long enough to read?
