# Wrapped 2025 Is Here — Spotify (2025)

**Why it's in the set:** pure kinetic typography in 9:16 for the biggest annual social moment in music (≈ 53 M views on this regional upload at time of research, *source*: YouTube Shorts listing): the "visual mixtape" look (xerox grain, stripe bars, overlapping wordmarks, colour swaps) cut at a frantic rate. Reference for type-only vertical motion.

## The idea
2025 Wrapped as a "modern 'visual mixtape'" inspired by handmade mixtapes and burned CDs, balancing "chaos and clarity", with "a reduced color palette", gradients and textures, analog mixed with digital (*source*: Spotify newsroom, 3 Dec 2025; Jeremy Wirth quote). The Short is the launch announcement: "It's time… Go get your Wrapped!" (*source*: description, published 3 Dec 2025).

## Structure and pacing
- 9:16 confirmed (*source*: oEmbed 113×200); uploaded by Spotify India (official regional channel).
- Frames: hq1 (25 %): off-white ground, "Wrappe[d]" in heavy black lowercase on a white band across black/white vertical bar-code stripes, an outlined lime-green "2025" overlapping behind/through the word; hq3 (75 %): same lock-up on dark charcoal with "2025" in red; oardefault: "**Wrapped** is here." (bold + regular weights) at top with a hand-scribbled white underline, the big lock-up at lower half with stripe blocks stepping down at right, black ground with xerox speckle.
- *est.* median shot/state ≈ 0.3–0.6 s: the lock-up re-composes in colourway after colourway (green, red…) on the beat.

## Typography
- Spotify's brand sans (Spotify Mix, a geometric grotesk *est.* from the double-storey "a" and round "e"), extra-bold, tight tracking, lowercase "Wrapped".
- "2025" set in a contrasting rounded/outlined style that overlaps the wordmark (layered type, not side-by-side).
- Secondary line mixes bold and regular weights in one sentence; a hand-drawn scribble underline is the "human" mark.

## Colour and light
Reduced palette per state: black, off-white (#EDEBE6 *est.*), and one accent (lime green #A4E30F *est.*, red #E8383D *est.*) swapped between states; heavy photocopy grain/speckle and slightly rough edges; no lighting, no gradients in these frames.

## Motion techniques
- Kinetic type re-compositions: the same lock-up recoloured and re-scaled; stripe/bar-code blocks wipe and step in.
- Frame-within-frame: the 9:16 frame contains a scaled copy of the lock-up with large blurred/dimmed duplicates at the sides (hq1/hq3), i.e. echoes at different scales.
- Grain and xerox texture over everything (analog feel).

## Transitions
Hard cuts and colour swaps on the beat (*est.*).

## Sound design and music
Music-led (*est.*); not documented.

## What makes it world-class
1. Type is the entire visual: one wordmark lock-up, re-composed many ways, carries a global campaign.
2. Strict reduced palette with one swappable accent; each swap is a beat.
3. Texture (xerox grain, scribble) gives analog warmth that matches the "mixtape" concept.
4. Layered/overlapping type (2025 through "Wrapped") instead of tidy stacking: deliberate tension between chaos and clarity.
5. Vertical composition uses top text + lower lock-up + stepped stripe blocks, filling 9:16 without dead space.

## What Anikit would need
- `text` with tight `letterSpacing`, weight mix via inline spans; stroke-only text (`stroke` with `color: "none"`) for the outlined "2025"; `shape` stripe blocks via `mograph` `repeat`; `mask` wipes; colour swaps via `animate` on `@beat`; `component` "grain" + `post` texture; `audio` beat markers.
- GAP: hand-drawn scribble stroke and photocopy edge texture.

## Judge's checklist
- Is type the only visual element, with one lock-up recomposed rather than many unrelated titles?
- Is the palette reduced to black/off-white + one accent per state, swapping on the beat?
- Is there analog texture (grain, rough edges) that is visible at 100 % crop but does not blur letterforms?
- Does overlapping/layered type stay legible at phone size?
- Do state changes occur every ≤ 0.7 s on the beat (frantic) without a single dead frame?
