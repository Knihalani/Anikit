# TNT Sports brand and broadcast system — DixonBaxi (2023)

**Why it's in the set: a full sports-network rebrand (BT Sport became TNT Sports) whose entire motion system runs through one framing device, The Viewfinder, made from the logo's two Ts; the reference for an editorial, fan-first sports package, as opposed to chrome-and-flares.**

URL note: the oEmbed-verified upload is DixonBaxi's 12 s "TNT_CLOCK_00003" (author "DixonBaxi", uploaded 2023-09-12), one element of the package (a countdown clock). The full system is shown on DixonBaxi's own case-study page (alt URL), which embeds several motion files and stills. Judge against both.

## The idea
"Right by the fans": TNT Sports "flips the traditional broadcast model, shifting power from studios to supporters" (*source*: DixonBaxi). The team filmed real fans (in the stands, at the pub, on the sofa, pitchside), not actors (*source*). At the heart is **The Viewfinder**, "a dynamic graphic device born from the two Ts in the logo" that frames sport on and off the pitch, "magnifying emotion, sparking opinion, and spotlighting data", driven by "a set of motion behaviours" (*source*).

## Structure and pacing
Package elements include a countdown clock (this upload), "off-clock" data moments and player-stat walkthroughs (*source*: DixonBaxi). The clock is a loop-style bumper; broader promos cut fast between fan footage framed by the Viewfinder (*est.* median ~1 s).

## Typography
A system "developed with F37" pairing a bold sans with an expressive serif, so the brand can move between "data-driven precision and emotional storytelling" (*source*).

## Colour and light
High-contrast brand colours behind raw, documentary fan footage (*est.* from case-study stills); "warm, tactile identity" (*source*).

## Motion techniques
The Viewfinder opens, crops and reframes footage and data; "gestural motion to navigate the graphic system"; an "editorial approach to data" with immersive UI for match stats (*source*). A walkthrough clip ("tnt_formations_football_walkthrough-facts-stats") shows formation and stat graphics (*source*: asset name on the case page).

## Transitions
The Viewfinder frame itself is the transition: it scales, crops and travels to reveal the next shot or data card (*est.*).

## Sound design and music
Not documented in sources read.

## What makes it world-class
1. One device (the logo's two Ts as a viewfinder) frames everything: footage, opinion, data.
2. Real fans and documentary texture instead of glossy CG.
3. Data treated editorially, as typographic layouts with hierarchy, not as tables.
4. Two-voice typography (sans for facts, serif for emotion) gives range inside one system.

## What Anikit would need
- `mask`/`clip` shapes as the Viewfinder over `video`, `text` in two families, `box` layouts for stat cards, `component` charts for data, `camera` reframing, `transitions`.
- Mostly existing features; the gap is authored fan footage.

## Judge's checklist
- Is there one framing device that crops, reveals and carries data across the whole package?
- Do data graphics have editorial hierarchy (one big number, supporting detail)?
- Is footage real and textured rather than stock-glossy?
- Does the type switch roles (precise vs emotional) with a clear rule?
- Does a 12 s element like the clock stay on-system and readable at a glance?
