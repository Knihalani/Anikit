# FIFA Men's World Cup 2026 | FOX Sports — Drive Studio (2026)

**Why it's in the set: a full US network tournament package from the specialist sports-broadcast studio (Drive also did FOX's 2018 and 2019 World Cup packages); the reference for team-templated 3D graphics with real fan texture.**

oEmbed: Drive Studio, 84 s, uploaded 2026-07-01. Alt: the virtual-set film (50 s).

## The idea
"FANTHEM": a fan's-eye view from inside the stands instead of "the world coming together", built from chants, scarves, typography, sound and supporter energy (*source*: Stash). Each country's chant, scarf and team pattern becomes an AR element in the virtual set as the tournament progresses, matched by the broadcast graphics (*source*). FIFA's "26" mark recurs through the package and sets (*source*: NewscastStudio via search summary).

## Structure and pacing
A package reel: open, team/player line-ups, transitions/bumpers, virtual set (*est.*). Player and team graphics use flag backgrounds and big country names (e.g. "ARGENTINA") (*source*: Stash captions). Median shot *est.* 1–1.5 s for an 84 s package reel.

## Typography
Bold, large condensed display caps for country and player names (*source* for bold large names; *est.* condensed). Typeface not named in sources read.

## Colour and light
Per-team palettes (Mexico green/white/red stripes; Argentina sky blue/white with sun) over a FOX blue and gold geometric frame; vivid red FIFA "26" panels (*source*: Stash captions). CG lit with specular sweeps across shapes and trophy (*est.*).

## Motion techniques
3D geometric shapes framing waving flags, trophy hero shots, scarves and patterns (*source*: Stash captions). The studio set renders on 32 Pixotope XR engines driving an LED volume (*source*: Pixotope via search summary). Cloth simulation for scarves/flags and camera orbits around 3D type (*est.*).

## Transitions
Branded wipes made of scarf pattern / geometric shapes sweeping the frame (*est.*); hits on music accents.

## Sound design and music
FOX reworked its broadcast theme in a more anthemic arrangement with added crowd noise (*source*: NewscastStudio via search summary).

## What makes it world-class
1. One concept (the fan in the stands) supplies every texture: scarves, chants, patterns.
2. Templated for 48 teams yet each variant feels specific (flag, pattern, chant).
3. 3D type and objects are lit with real speculars and depth, not flat extrusions.
4. Graphics, AR and set share one system, so cuts between them feel continuous.

## What Anikit would need
- `scene3d` 3D type and geometry, `camera` orbits, `mograph` pattern repeats, `mask` wipes, `motionBlur`, `post`, `audio` beat markers; `defs`/`with` for team variants.
- GAP: cloth simulation for flags/scarves; real-time AR/virtual set integration is out of scope.

## Judge's checklist
- Is a single concept visible in every element (here, fan culture), not just a palette?
- Are 3D letters lit with speculars and readable at broadcast safe-area sizes?
- Do variants (teams) swap colours/patterns without breaking layout?
- Do transitions use branded shapes and land on music hits?
- Do names and numbers stay legible at full speed (no motion-blur smear on text holds)?
