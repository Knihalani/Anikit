# OFFFMX 2025 Main Titles — XK Studio (2025)

**Why it's in the set: OFFF main titles are the motion-design world's annual showcase commission; XK Studio's 2025 Mexico City titles build the whole sequence around hands as the symbol of making.**

oEmbed: author "OFFF Festival", 99 s.

## The idea
Hands as the central element, "symbolizing artistic creation and the human gesture behind ideas"; "a tribute to experimentation and to the digital narratives that redefine today's creative landscape" (*source*: Stash, quoting OFFF).

## Structure and pacing
Main-titles format: a sequence of CG vignettes with speaker/credit names, ending on OFFFMX branding (*est.*). Median shot *est.* 1–2 s over 99 s.

## Typography
Speaker names and event title as supers (*est.*); typeface not named in sources read.

## Colour and light
Not documented; *est.* rich, saturated studio-lit CG.

## Motion techniques
CG motion experiment (*source*: Stash tags "3D Style, CG"). Hands performing gestures of making; expect macro framing, soft-body/deformation and shallow DOF (*est.*).

## Transitions
*est.* gesture-to-gesture match cuts between vignettes.

## Sound design and music
Sound by **Zelig Sound** (*source*: Stash).

## What makes it world-class
1. One motif (the hand) unifies every vignette.
2. Human gesture rendered with care: hands are the hardest thing to make convincing in CG.
3. Titles as craft showcase: each shot is a self-contained still worth posting.

## What Anikit would need
- `scene3d`, `camera` macro moves with DOF, `text` supers, `motionBlur`, `post`, `audio`.
- GAP: rigged, skin-shaded hands and deformation.

## Judge's checklist
- Is there one visual motif repeated with variation across the whole sequence?
- Does each vignette read as a finished still with deliberate lighting?
- Do gestures have believable weight and timing (no linear/floaty moves)?
- Are titles sized and placed consistently, with room in the composition?
