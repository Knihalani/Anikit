# DEMO trailer — Studio Dumbar/DEPT (2026, for DEMO 2027)

**Why it's in the set: the trailer for the world's largest motion-design festival, by the studio that founded it; the reference for a type-only, system-driven identity that is legible and wild at the same time.**

oEmbed: author "STUDIO DUMBAR/DEPT®", 68 s, uploaded 2026-05-05 (timed with the 2027 open call, *source*: It's Nice That 18 May 2026). The 2027 trailer itself is not described in sources I read, so specifics below come from the documented DEMO system (2019–2025) and are marked.

## The idea
DEMO replaces advertising screens with 24 hours of non-commercial motion design (*source*: It's Nice That). First edition 7 Nov 2019 on 80 screens in Amsterdam Centraal; 2025 ran in 15 cities across 7 countries; 5,000+ submissions in 2025 (*source*: Studio Dumbar; It's Nice That).

## Structure and pacing
Typographic trailer cut to sound, announcing the call for entries (*est.*; median shot *est.* < 1.5 s).

## Typography
The identity is built on a **variable typeface whose shape changes via a self-developed script**; any word gets its own animated character, stays legible, and the logo can be distorted by the cursor on the site (*source*: Studio Dumbar, DEMO 2019). Logo and typography stay the core across editions (*source*: DEMO 2025).

## Colour and light
One brand colour per edition completing RGB: red '19, green '22, blue '25 (*source*: DEMO 2025 page); 2019 also used orange and dark blue (*source*: DEMO 2019 page). Flat, high-contrast (*est.*). The 2027 colour is not documented.

## Motion techniques
Script-driven glyph distortion, stretching/squashing letters across full-screen formats (portrait station screens and landscape) (*source* for script; formats *est.*). 2025 added a "playful and distorted globe" and 3D walking DEMO letters in the online DEMOVERSE (*source*). Motion made in Cavalry with a shared base setup (*source*: Cavalry Medium post title/summary via search).

## Transitions
Type morphs and colour swaps between words, no crossfades (*est.*).

## Sound design and music
A sonic identity is part of the brief; 2025 contrasted "ethereal and spacious sounds" with "heavier and textural ones", with a sound library built from motion tests (*source*: DEMO 2025).

## What makes it world-class
1. Type-only and never boring: distortion is extreme yet legibility is held.
2. A procedural system (script + variable font) generates endless on-brand variations.
3. One flat brand colour per edition: instant recognition across 15 cities.
4. Sound designed from the motion tests, so they are bound together.

## What Anikit would need
- `text` with `split`/`kinetic`, `mograph` repeats, `shape` masks, hard `transitions`, `audio` markers.
- GAP: variable-font axis animation and per-glyph scripted deformation (stretch, bulge) beyond scale/skew.

## Judge's checklist
- Is every frame type-driven, with letters distorted yet readable?
- Is distortion systematic (same rules each time), not random wobble?
- Is the palette one flat brand colour plus black/white?
- Do glyph hits land on sound events?
- Does it work in both landscape and portrait framings?
