# A TISZÁN - Annecy Festival 2025 — GOBELINS Paris (2025)

**Why it's in the set: each year GOBELINS students make the short films shown before Annecy screenings; they are the festival's on-screen identity and a reference for complete micro-stories in under a minute.**

## The idea
The 2025 country of honour was Hungary; all nine GOBELINS films drew on Hungarian culture (*source*: 3DVF). *A Tiszán*: as the river thaws at winter's end, Ottó reopens his small ferry across the Tisza for villagers in the Hungarian countryside (*source*: 3DVF synopsis).

## Structure and pacing
A single micro-narrative: setup (thaw), action (crossing), payoff, then festival sign-off (*est.*). Calm pacing (*est.* median 2–4 s).

1. A complete story with character and place in under 60 s.
