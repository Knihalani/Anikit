# MOTION PLUS DESIGN TOKYO - OPENER — Studio River (2024)

**Why it's in the set: a conference opener with its own narrative and a fully authored CG character (a fabric-skinned humanoid), made by a small Paris studio; the reference for an event opener that is a short film, not a speaker sizzle.**

oEmbed: author "Studio River", 133 s. Alt: the Motion Awards cut uploaded by Motionographer (133 s).

## The idea
Motion Plus Design Tokyo usually opens with a fast speaker trailer; Studio River built an opener "with its own identity" about AI: "the creative entity of the future, a kind of humanoid combining AI technologies with the organic perfection of the human brain" (*source*: Motion Awards entry). It also carries event info (date, location, sponsors) (*source*: Stash).

## Structure and pacing
Character-led short with city and train environments (*source*: credits list "City / Train modeling"). Planned at ~1 min but grew because the emotion needed time (*source*: Stash). Shots are longer than a sizzle (*est.* median 2–3 s), ending on titles.

## Typography
Title design and animation by **Pierrick Selva** (*source*); event info set as clean supers over CG (*est.*).

## Colour and light
Cool, dark urban night with practical lights on the city and train, soft key on the fabric character (*est.* from thumbnail and the city/train scenes).

## Motion techniques
Full CG: character concept by Barnabé Cantin; fabric character design/model/animation by Paul Maurin; fabric shading by Alexandre Scariot; simulations credited (*source*). Expect cloth sim on the character, shallow depth of field, slow dolly moves (*est.*).

## Transitions
Cuts and camera-motivated transitions between character and city (*est.*).

## Sound design and music
Music composed in-house by **Alexandre Scariot**; sound design and mix by Nourian and Scariot; composing in-house gave control over highlights and edit (*source*: Stash; Motion Awards credits).

## What makes it world-class
1. An event opener with a point of view (how creatives feel about AI), not a logo spin.
2. A bespoke character with a distinctive material (fabric) rather than a stock humanoid.
3. Music and edit authored together, so hits land where the picture needs them.
4. Event info integrated as designed titles inside the film.

## What Anikit would need
- `scene3d` for environments, `camera` moves with `depth`/DOF, `text` supers, `post`, `motionBlur`, `audio` with markers.
- GAP: character rigging/animation and cloth simulation; environment modelling.

## Judge's checklist
- Does the opener have an idea or question, beyond showing the event name?
- Is there a hero subject with a distinctive material that holds at 100% crop?
- Is depth of field/lighting used to direct the eye in each shot?
- Are event titles designed into the frame rather than pasted as lower thirds?
- Do music accents and cuts coincide?
