# Ye Kou Si Kuo — Lola Lefèvre / Miyu (2024)

**Why it's in the set: the current top of commissioned 2D animation (Annecy's 2025 Cristal). A Dayglo palette, a chunky expressive line and objects that come alive make a small domestic story feel like a rave.**

## The idea
"After a wild party in their flat, three hungover friends get ready for the next one, while jamming to their favorite band" (*source*: Annecy synopsis). Inanimate objects are animated as characters (*source*: Cartoon Brew).

## Structure and pacing
- 148 s (*source*: Annecy listing, "2 min 28 sec").
- Morning after, waking up hazy, then preparing for and launching the next party (*source*). Many close-ups and bold camera moves (*source*); shots about 1–2 s in the dance sections (*est.*, from the genre and the track tempo).

## Typography
None documented.

## Colour and light
"Dayglo rave palette" (*source*): fluorescent pinks, oranges, greens and blues on dark or saturated grounds (*est.* for hues).

## Motion techniques
"2D Computer" technique (*source*: Annecy). An "expressive chunky line" shared by characters and backgrounds; background line art fills the frame with a dense pattern "like a tapestry" (*source*: Cartoon Brew). Objects come to life (*source*).

## Transitions
Close-ups and camera moves carry the cuts (*source*); specific transitions not documented.

## Sound design and music
Naive New Beaters with Star Feminine Band (a Beninese girls' band): an upbeat dance track; character and object motion on the beat (*est.*).

## What makes it world-class
1. A unified graphic language: the same heavy line in characters and backgrounds, so the frame reads as one illustration in motion.
2. A palette that is loud (Dayglo) yet controlled, with deliberate contrast.
3. Inventive animation of objects: the domestic world becomes part of the dance.
4. Intimate, energetic camera (close-ups, bold moves) instead of flat side-on staging.

## What Anikit would need
`image` sequences for drawn frames, `shape` patterns, `camera` moves, `audio` beat sync. GAPs: frame-by-frame 2D character animation; animated pattern-filled line-art backgrounds.

## Judge's checklist
- Do characters and backgrounds share one line and colour language?
- Is a loud palette controlled (a few dominant hues, clear figure-ground)?
- Do non-character objects get personality through motion?
- Does the camera get close and move with intent, rather than staging everything flat?
- Does motion lock to the beat in the dance sections?
