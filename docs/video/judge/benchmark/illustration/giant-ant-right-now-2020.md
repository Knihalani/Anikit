# Right Now — Giant Ant (2020)

**Why it's in the set: a short, emotional illustrated film made of simple, specific vignettes of people around the world in 2020; it shows how much feeling hard cuts between well-observed moments can carry.**

## The idea
"If there's one thing we've learned in 2020, it's that things are hard… and that we're in it together" (*source*: Giant Ant). Written by co-founder Jay Grandin in March 2020 as Covid spread (*source*: Stash).

## Structure and pacing
- 106 s (*source*: Vimeo oEmbed).
- A montage of vignettes in different places (still filenames include New York, a church, Tanzania, a courtyard, *source*: studio page image names), joined by hard cuts. Grandin wanted "hard cuts" so that the film could be split into pieces among the studio's 19 home offices (*source*: Stash).
- About 20–30 shots, median about 3–4 s (*est.*, from the length and the vignette structure).

## Typography
None in the body (*est.*).

## Colour and light
Flat colour cleanup; "Next time, we'll likely use fewer than the 67 colours shown in this legend" (*source*: studio page, character colour palette legend). Warm, soft palettes per location (*est.*, from stills).

## Motion techniques
Frame-by-frame character animation with flat colour applied frame by frame in cleanup (*source*); illustrated backgrounds by five illustrators (*source*). Small, observed human gestures (*est.*).

## Transitions
Hard cuts (*source*), likely with graphic or action matches between vignettes (*est.*).

## Sound design and music
Music and sound by Ambrose Yu (*source*).

## What makes it world-class
1. Observation: each vignette is a specific human moment, not a generic "person on phone".
2. One coherent illustration style across five illustrators and seven animators.
3. A large, controlled character palette (67 colours) gives skin, clothes and light variety without breaking the style.
4. Hard cuts on emotional beats: the edit, not camera tricks, carries the feeling.

## What Anikit would need
`image` layers for illustrated backgrounds and characters, `camera` moves, `transitions` (`cut`), `audio`. GAP: frame-by-frame character animation.

## Judge's checklist
- Is each shot a specific, observed moment that reads in under 3 s?
- Is the illustration style consistent across all shots?
- Are the colours varied but harmonised (a deliberate palette system)?
- Do the cuts fall on emotional or musical beats?
