# Taste canon: sources

Every claim in [`TASTE.md`](TASTE.md) traces to one of these. Research was done in November 2026 from primary sources where they could be reached: the practitioners' own words, standards documents, and peer-reviewed studies. Where only a summary could be fetched, the entry says so, and so do the caveats below. Thresholds marked *(heuristic)* in TASTE.md are our proposals built on these sources, not published standards; calibrate them against stills the user accepts and rejects.

## §1 Typography

1. *The Vignelli Canon*, Massimo Vignelli (Lars Müller 2010; free PDF via RIT Vignelli Center). https://www.rit.edu/vignellicenter/sites/rit.edu.vignellicenter/files/documents/The%20Vignelli%20Canon.pdf. Supports: four typefaces; 1–2 sizes; 2× contrast; leading table; vulgarity vs strength; white space; colour as signifier.
2. Bringhurst, *Elements of Typographic Style* §3.1.1 "Don't compose without a scale", via webtypography.net. http://webtypography.net/3.1.1. Supports: use a modular/traditional scale with distinct intervals.
3. Tim Brown, "More Meaningful Typography", *A List Apart*. https://alistapart.com/article/more-meaningful-typography/. Supports: modular scales, 1.618 example, scale as guide not dogma.
4. Müller-Brockmann, "Grid and Design Philosophy" (from *Grid Systems in Graphic Design*). https://neugraphic.com/muller-brockmann/muller-brockmann-text2.html. Supports: grid as an ordering attitude.
5. Saul Bass, interview with Pamela Haskin, *Film Quarterly* 1996, via Wikiquote. https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Saul_Bass. Supports: titles set mood and express the core story metaphorically.
6. Art of the Title, *Se7en* (Kyle Cooper). https://www.artofthetitle.com/title/se7en/. Supports: motivated handmade type mixed with Helvetica; kept accidents.
7. Art of the Title, *True Detective* (Patrick Clair/Elastic). https://www.artofthetitle.com/title/true-detective/. Supports: story-first concept; double exposure as meaning.
8. EBU R95, *Safe Areas for 16:9 Television Production*. https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/r/r095.pdf. Supports: action-safe 3.5%, graphics-safe 5%.
9. BBC Subtitle Guidelines. https://www.bbc.co.uk/accessibility/forproducts/guides/subtitles/. Supports: 160–180 wpm; 0.3 s/word; line height 7–8% (player 7–9%); 0.5° fallback; 37 chars broadcast / 68% width online; white-on-black; colour list; 120% line height.
10. Bringhurst §2.1.2 measure, via webtypography.net. http://webtypography.net/2.1.2. Supports: 45–75 chars, 66 ideal, 40–50 multi-column.
11. Netflix English (USA) Timed Text Style Guide. https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/217350977-English-Timed-Text-Style-Guide. Supports: 42 chars/line; 2 lines; 20 cps adult / 17 cps children; white proportional sans.
12. Netflix Timed Text Style Guide: General Requirements. https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215758617-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-General-Requirements. Supports: min 5/6 s, max 7 s per event.
13. BBC R&D, "How fast should subtitles be?" (mirror of bbc.co.uk/rd/blog/2015-09-how-fast-should-subtitles-be). https://bbcbreakingnews.pages.dev/rd/blog/2015-09-how-fast-should-subtitles-be. Supports: Ofcom 160–180 wpm; over 200 wpm difficult.
14. Rasmus Andersson, Inter "Dynamic Metrics" (archived). https://d.rsms.me/inter-website/v3/dynmetrics/. Supports: tracking formula; negative tracking at display sizes.
15. Bringhurst §2.1.6 letterspacing, via webtypography.net. http://webtypography.net/2.1.6. Supports: caps/small caps +5–10%; don't letterspace lowercase.
16. W3C WCAG 2.2. https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/. Supports: 4.5:1 / 3:1 / 7:1; large text 18 pt or 14 pt bold; 3:1 non-text.
17. APCA in a Nutshell (Myndex). https://git.apcacontrast.com/documentation/APCA_in_a_Nutshell. Supports: Lc 90/75/60/45/30/15 with size and weight pairs.
18. RTINGS, "Chroma Subsampling" (with TestUFO chroma test https://testufo.com/chroma). https://www.rtings.com/tv/learn/chroma-subsampling. Supports: 4:2:0 blurs coloured text on colour; luma edges stay sharp.

## §5 Motion

- **[L87]** John Lasseter, "Principles of Traditional Animation Applied to 3D Computer Animation," SIGGRAPH '87, *Computer Graphics* 21(4). - **[KOWALSKI]** Emil Kowalski skills, stagger intervals (mirror), https://instagit.com/emilkowalski/skills/staggered-animations-delay-intervals/. Supports 30 to 80 ms per item (heuristic).
