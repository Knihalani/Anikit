# Storyboarder

For each beat give: id, **what the viewer understands** after it, hold, the cut in (and why: on action, on a beat, a match, a hard contrast), the focal element, and the layers in z-order with their motion. Taste canon: `anikit-video docs taste`.

- **Hold:** entrance + `words/3 + 1` s for text (≈ 0.3 s per word + 0.5–1 s); a single word on a beat can read in 0.4–0.6 s. Shot lengths follow the declared `intent` (fast: 0.5–1.5 s, none over 2.5 s; steady: up to 6 s; calm: long holds that stay alive), and **vary**: a run of short shots, then a hold on a downbeat. Equal lengths everywhere is a metronome.
- **Framing:** vary shot size and placement beat to beat (wide → close → insert; left third → full bleed → right third). Centre only when the beat is a statement or the world is symmetric by design. Give moving subjects lead room; shape the empty space.
- **Scale:** one element dominates (≥ 3:1 against the next); type sizes come from 2–4 levels on one ratio.
- **Motion by role, not by preset:** heavy things anticipate and settle; light things overshoot a little; text arrives fast and then **stays still and sharp** while it is read. One thing leads; the rest follow 0.1–0.4 s later. Pick 2–3 entrance behaviours that mean something (a word that *builds* is built; a word that *breaks* breaks), not fade-and-rise on everything.
- **Cuts:** cut on action or on the beat; carry a shape, colour or direction across a cut (match cut) at least once; keep eye-trace continuous (the next focal point near the last). Transitions only when motivated.
- **Depth:** 2D flat space and 3D deep space are both valid; choose per beat for meaning and don't mix them by accident.
- **Formats:** 9:16 and 1:1 stack the subject and type; keep the top 12% and bottom 20% clear on 9:16.

Take the vocabulary from the concept first; a look (`anikit-video looks <name>`) or template is a quick start to override, never the design.
