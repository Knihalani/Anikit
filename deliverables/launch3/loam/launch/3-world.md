# 3 (pass 2) · One world

(Pass 1's world is kept in `pass1-docs/3-world.md`.)

**Material:** the same real home windowsill, rebuilt richer: a painted chalk sill; a **deep sage-green limewash wall** (polyhaven beige_wall_001 tinted #8FB5AA, reading dark green in shadow); real potted plants (polyhaven potted_plant_01/02/04: terracotta, pedestal terracotta, glazed white with gravel). The new material is **rain**: thin lit streaks (scene3d particles with `stretch` and `glint`) that catch the sun.

**Key light:** hard low morning sun through a four-pane window (rig `window`, angle ~60°, height ~24°): crisp mullion bars across wall and sill, deep shadow between the panes. It is the light of the whole film. The rain is only visible where it crosses a sun pane — it sparkles in the light, vanishes in the shadow bars.

**Palette family:** deep sage-green wall, chalk sill, terracotta, leaf green, warm sun; the rain is silver-white. No other accent.

**Type voice:** Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold (`target` text for the particles) — round, friendly, ink-trapped, chunky enough to be built from drops. Only one type moment: the name made of rain, then one short line in the same face. No floor captions.

**Sound world:** acoustic guitar and felt piano at 96 bpm; rain on leaves and terracotta as foley (sound auto + the rain's own hiss); one drop; ring out.

**Anikit means:** scene3d only: `window` rig, polyhaven models and plaster, particles for rain (`over`, lit, `glint`, `stretch`) and for the name (`target` text).

**Changes shot to shot:** subject (arrowhead in rain / dry succulent / sensor in wet soil / pedestal drizzle / whole sill / the name), scale (macro gravel ↔ whole sill), camera (85 mm eye level, low 100 mm macro, high 50 mm top-down, slow crane).
