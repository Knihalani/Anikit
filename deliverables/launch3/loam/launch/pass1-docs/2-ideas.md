# 2 · Four ideas

The last Loam film (deliverables/launch/loam) was an illustrated cut-away pot where water stopped at the roots; it scored 4.56 and failed. Its rejects were B (flood comedy) and D (pot weather). This film must not be a cut-away or an illustration.

### A. The sun reads it out
Morning sun crosses a real windowsill and the light itself writes each plant's answer on the sill beside it, cast as shadow lettering: "Not yet." by the succulent, "Now." by the thirsty one, "Sunday." by the fern. The windowsill becomes the app.
- Literal test (delete "Loam" and "sensor"): "the morning light on your windowsill tells each plant, in words, when and how much to drink." Survives: it is a picture of the *answer*, not the mechanism.
- Swap test: a generic reminder app gives everyone the same schedule; this film's whole joke is that every plant on the same sill gets a *different* answer, which only reading each pot's soil gives. Passes, with the sensor shown as the cause once.

### B. The flood (comedy, reject from last time)
A tiny haworthia under a tidal wave from a watering can. Funny once; stops at "don't overwater". Fails swap (any plant app), can't escalate to the product.

### C. The bartender
A bartender's jigger pours each plant its exact measure, like a cocktail order: "a double for the monstera, nothing for the cactus". Wit, but it is about the pour, not the knowing; the props (jiggers, bar) pull the world away from the home. Swap: weak.

### D. Out there: the plants' timetable
The sill as a railway departure board where plants are trains and waterings are departures. Literal-pun risk (a split-flap board was already rejected as "a literal pun") and it puts type on a board instead of in the world. Discard.

## Pick: A — the sun reads it out
**Device in one sentence:** because the soil is read, the sunlight on your windowsill writes each plant its own answer — when, and how much.

**Escalation:**
1. Hook: a sentence of shadow slides across the sill and settles beside a succulent: "Not yet."
2. A thirsty plant, low angle: the light says "Now." in letters bigger than the pot.
3. The cause, macro: the sensor clipped into the soil; the light writes the amount: "Half a cup."
4. It spreads: quick cuts along the sill, each plant a different answer ("Sunday.", "Just a splash.").
5. Hard stop: a cloud crosses the sun; the words go out; silence.
6. Payoff: the sun returns over the whole sill and the light writes **loam** across it.
7. CTA in the same light.

**Ending:** the name is written by the same window light that has been talking all film.

**Why it won:** it is the only idea where the product's *output* (a different answer per plant) is the image, the world is the real place the product lives (a sunlit sill), and the device can grow from one word to a whole sill to the name.

Rejects kept: B (flood), C (bartender).
