# 3 · One world

**Material:** a real windowsill at home. A painted sill (warm off-white eggshell, #ECE8DF), a sage limewash plaster wall behind it (polyhaven beige_wall_001 tinted #A9BBA2), and real potted plants (polyhaven potted_plant_01/02/04: terracotta, a pedestal pot, a glazed white pot with gravel). The sensor is a small white plastic clip-stake, the only made object. Everything at 100 % crop is photographed texture, not primitives.

**Key light:** low morning sun through the window, from camera-right and above (rig `window`, angle ≈ 60°, height ≈ 20°), slightly warm, hard enough to cast crisp shadows of the pots across the sill. The sun is the narrator: every word in the film is a shadow it casts (scene3d `gobo`, a card in front of the key, so letters fall on the sill and wrap over anything in the way). One cloud passes before the payoff (the hard stop): key down, words gone.

**Palette family:** sage wall, chalk sill, terracotta, leaf green, warm sunlight; shadows a cool grey-green. No accent colour: the device's accent is *shadow vs. sun*.

**Type voice:** Archivo Black — a heavy, blunt grotesque that survives being cast as a soft-edged shadow at an angle (thin or high-contrast faces dissolve in the penumbra; Schibsted Grotesk's variable outlines left holes in the cast N). Type lives *in* the world: it is never overlaid, it is light and shadow on the sill. One answer per plant, at a time.

**Sound world:** acoustic: felt piano and nylon guitar plucks at 96 bpm, soft shaker; room tone; a ceramic clink as the sensor goes into the pot; a soft breath of wind as the cloud passes; one water drop before the name.

**Anikit means:** `scene3d` only (ray-traced, polyhaven models and plaster, ambientcg-free painted floor), text gobos, the `window` rig; generated acoustic music + `sound: auto`.

**Changes shot to shot:** subject (succulent / terracotta arrowhead / the sensor in soil / pedestal plant / the whole sill), scale (macro gravel ↔ the whole sill from above), camera (low eye-level 85 mm, top-down, macro 100 mm, slow lateral track, rising crane).

## Style frames (`frames/frames-sheet.png`)
Same key moment ("Now." cast beside the terracotta plant), varying the material of sill and wall:
- **A · oak sill, beige plaster** — warm, pleasant; the wood grain and knots fight the letters.
- **B · painted chalk sill, sage wall** — the shadow letters read cleanest, the terracotta pops against the sage; the frame is all sun and shadow.
- **C · walnut sill, terracotta wall** — rich but monochrome: the pot disappears into the wall, and the warm-dark ground is a rejected look.

**Picked B:** the shadow word is the hero and B is the only surface where the light's handwriting reads like print. Fixes forward: lift the wall to a lighter sage, use Archivo Black (static outlines), angle cameras higher when a word must be read so letters aren't foreshortened.
