# 1 · The promise

**Promise (≤ 15 words, no name):** Every plant on your sill gets told exactly when and how much to drink.

**Mode: teaser-leaning proof.** The answer the app gives (when, how much) is the thing we see from frame 0; the sensor itself appears once, as the cause, at ~3 s; the name lands at the end.

**Not in this film** (end card at most, or nowhere):
- that it is an app on a phone (no phone UI is shown: we don't have the real UI, and inventing one breaks the honesty rule);
- how the sensor works (moisture, readings, charts);
- any numbers, plant counts, battery, price, platforms.
