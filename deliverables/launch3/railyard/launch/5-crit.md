## Gaps (verbatim) → kind

- [CRAFT (type)] The type is the weakest part. Set the logo with intent and make it physical (branded, cut or inked into the wood), or let the resonance itself form the wordmark
- [IDEA (no choreography / device doesn't develop)] There is no choreography. Build a chain reaction where one strike sets off the whole field and shows 'one call, everything settles' through causal motion instead of repeated static macro shots
- [WORLD] The light and materials are inconsistent. Use one coherent light source, believable steel and wood shading, and a palette chosen on purpose beyond grey plus one red accent
- [CRAFT (type)] Typography needs a point of view. Custom or carefully chosen letterforms that resonate, vibrate or are cut or branded into the wood would make the type part of the idea.
- [IDEA (literal)] Push the metaphor past literal: show sympathetic resonance spreading through the field as visible choreography (fork to fork, wave across the room) with scale and camera variation, not repeated tine close-ups.
- [WORLD (stock gobo, generic material)] Replace the stock gobo-shadow look with motivated light and real material detail (worn wood, fingerprints, imperfect chrome, varied blocks) so the frames work as posters.

## Tells (verbatim) → kind

- [IDEA] The metaphor (tuning forks resonating = 'settled instantly') is a reasonable start but obvious, and it never develops past forks on blocks
- [WORLD (gobo repeated)] Window-blind shadow bands are reused in almost every frame as a stock atmosphere effect, and the shadow direction and shape shift from shot to shot with no motivation
- [WORLD/STRUCTURE (repeated close-ups)] Repeated near-identical close-ups of fork tines (frames 1 and 5); little happens across the sequence
- [WORLD (generic CG)] Objects are generic CG: plain wooden blocks with a procedural wood texture, a red ball striker, a field of forks with inconsistent scale and placement, which suggests generated or template output
- [CRAFT (end card type)] The end card is default bold grotesk in flat red pasted onto the wood, with a low-contrast tagline that is barely legible. It has no material integration (not engraved, burned or printed) and no typographic idea
- [CRAFT/IDEA (generic copy)] The end card uses a default heavy grotesk in flat red, stamped onto a wooden block like a slide. The tagline 'One API call. Settled instantly.' is generic SaaS copy, and the type has no relationship to the material it sits on.
- [IDEA (literal pun)] The metaphor is literal: tuning forks for 'in sync' or 'resonance'. It is a one-step visual pun, and the frames show little more than forks and blocks.
- [WORLD (stock gobo)] Venetian-blind and window-grid gobo shadows on a grey seamless are a stock 'cinematic' cliché. They repeat in almost every frame and do the work of a real lighting idea.
- [WORLD (CG-default materials)] The materials are CG-default: tiling wood texture, chrome forks with no handling marks, overlaid noise grain. The field of forks is near-instanced, with the same block over and over.
- [STRUCTURE (no escalation)] Three of the six frames are near-identical close-ups of fork tines against shadow stripes, so little happens and the sequence lacks escalation.
