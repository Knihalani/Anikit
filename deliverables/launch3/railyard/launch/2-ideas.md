# 2 · Four ideas

## A. Sympathetic forks
Strike a tuning fork in one room; its twin on a box across the hall is already ringing — no delay, no wire. Then two forks across a city, then a hall of a hundred forks all sounding the one strike.
- Literal test (delete name + "payments/API/settlement"): *you strike here and it sounds there, at the same instant.* Survives; nothing about rails, yards, money diagrams.
- Swap test: put a card network or a bank on it — it's about *simultaneity between two places*, not reach or price. Partial pass; the ring-at-every-change sound (state changes heard) makes it ours.

## B. Communicating vessels
Pour into one glass and the glass at the far end of the table rises at the same moment. Levels equalise across a row of fifty glasses.
- Literal: survives. Swap: "balance/levels" reads as generic finance; also close to last batch's liquid-in-tray film (anikit). Rejected for world repetition.

## C. Shadow before the hand (out there)
A hand reaches to place a coin; the coin's shadow is already on the far table before the hand moves — effect before cause.
- Literal: survives. Swap: reads as a magic trick, not money. Hard to make read in 1 s. Rejected.

## D. The relay without a handoff
Runners where the baton is already in the last hand. Drifts to tracks/rails = the name's pun (literal). Rejected.

## Pick: A, Sympathetic forks
**Device:** a strike on one fork makes its distant twin ring at the same instant — the money "sounds" on arrival.

**Escalation:**
1. Macro: a felt mallet strikes a steel fork on a walnut box (hint).
2. Cut wide: a second fork across the room — its tines are already blurring; water in a glass on its box shivers (the change, once).
3. Distance: the second fork is in another building, seen through a window; same instant.
4. Scale: a long table of forks; one strike, every one rings together.
5. Hard stop: a hand grips the tines; silence.
6. Payoff: one last strike — the ring settles into the name engraved on the box lid.

**Ending:** the name is engraved on the resonator box of the fork that answers; it lands as the final ring sounds.

**Why it won:** the device *is* the promise (send = arrive), it escalates by distance and count without leaving its world, and sound carries it — the score itself is the forks, so the soundtrack proves the idea. The webhook fact is implied (each ring = a state change heard) without a feature beat.

## E. Already settled (hourglass) — PICKED
An hourglass is turned over — and the sand is already at the bottom. No trickle, no wait: it lands settled.
- Literal test (delete name + payments/API): *you turn the glass and the sand has already run through.* Survives; it's a film about waiting disappearing, no rails, no diagrams.
- Swap test: "settled" is the payments word for money that has finished moving; a card network or a 2–3-day bank transfer can't claim it. The banking-hall world (marble teller counter, brass) ties it to money moving between bank accounts. Passes.
- **Device:** turning a glass, and the sand (the money, in cobalt) is already settled in the lower bulb the instant it lands.
- **Escalation:** one glass (hint) → a row on the counter turned on the beat, each lands settled (spreads) → macro: the sand surface still settling, the word acted out → a field of glasses turning in a chain-reaction wave across the counter (scale, choreography) → hard stop: one glass frozen mid-turn, silence → it lands; settled; the name is carved and gilded in the marble beside it.
- **Ending:** the last glass lands settled, and the camera settles with it onto the name inlaid in the counter.

## F. Lights across the street (rejected)
Flip a switch here, a window lights there. Swap test fails (any "instant" brand); close to forks' "here/there".

## G. The queue that isn't (rejected, out there)
