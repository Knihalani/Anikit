# 1 — The promise

**Promise (≤15 words, no name):** Wind it on Friday and it is still keeping time on Monday morning.

**Mode:** teaser — the full watch and its name are withheld until the last ~2.5 s. Before that we only see raking light passing over one dial, close.

**Source fact carried:** 72-hour power reserve (three days of light passing over the dial).

## Not in this film (end card at most)
- 38 mm steel case (end card, one line)
- guilloché dial (it is the *material* of the film, never captioned)
- 150 pieces (end card / call to action)
- Atelier Vauclair, independent Swiss maker (name on the dial at the end)
