**Mode:** proof. The real thing (a glass of the brewed coffee, then the bag) is on screen from frame 0; the reveal at the end is the name and the date, not the product.

**Audience:** Instagram Reels, specialty-coffee followers of a small roaster. Sound may be off: the film must read muted.
