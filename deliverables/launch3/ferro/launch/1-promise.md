# 1. The promise

**Real cast iron, finally light enough to toss with one hand.** (11 words)

Mode: **proof** — the pan is on screen from frame 0, and the thing it does (flip food into the air) is the proof.

Why this one: a 100-year pan was the last Ferro film's promise (a century of sunlight); it scored 4.19 and the jury
said a competitor could run it. "Half the weight" is the fact a classic-skillet owner actually feels, and it is
something only this pan can *do* on screen: a classic 3–4 kg skillet is never tossed.
