# 3. One world

- **Material:** seasoned cast iron (satin black, textured `Metal063`, imperfections) against a kitchen's breakfast —
  pancake, fried eggs, tomato, mushrooms. The food is the second material: soft, matte, warm, the opposite of iron.
  The ground is one seamless terracotta surface (a quarry-tile red, the floor of an English kitchen) that runs from
  counter to wall, so every angle — overhead, low, looking up into the air — is the same place.
- **Key light:** a hard morning sun through a kitchen window (`window` rig with window-pane gobo), high and from the
  left, warm-white. It gives every flying object a shadow on the counter — the shadow's distance is how we read height.
  Arc: same sun all film, a touch warmer at the end.
- **Palette family:** terracotta (#C4532E → #8E3418 in shadow), iron black (#1E1B19), cream (#F3EBDD) and yolk
  orange (#F09A12) — the yolk/pancake gold is the accent and lives only in the flying food.
- **Type voice:** Archivo at heavy weight, wide — the stamped, blunt grotesque of a foundry's pattern letters, not a
  Victorian serif (the last Ferro film used Besley). Type is *made of iron* and *tossed*: the claim floats in the air
  with the food at the top of the big toss, and the name is five iron letters thrown out of the pan onto the counter.
  Only the CTA is flat type, cream on the terracotta, under the letters.
- **Sound world:** acoustic band at 120 bpm (brushes, upright-ish bass, guitar strum) — a kitchen-radio morning;
  foley from the subject: iron on tile clunks, food landing in the pan (sizzle), whoosh of each toss; one bar of
  near-silence at the top of the hang, then everything lands on the downbeat.
- **Anikit means:** `scene3d` (lathe pan + tube handle, lathe food, extruded Archivo letters), `set: sweep`,
  `rig: window` with gobo, dense keyed ballistic motion, real DOF; 2D text only for the CTA.

## What changes shot to shot
Subject (egg → pancake → whole breakfast → words → letters), scale (macro rim → overhead → wide floor-to-air), and
camera (top-down, low macro, 3/4 high, low crane up, eye-level in the air).

## Style frames (launch/frames/compare.png)
1. pale concrete + window panes: strong light logic but grey, and half the frame is black backdrop.
2. **terracotta sweep: picked.** It is the only one that is a finished poster: one saturated ground, the black iron
   reads as a silhouette, the food glows against it, and it is unlike the last Ferro film's dark warm hearth.
3. oak + golden leaves: warm and murky, food and iron both disappear; too close to the rejected "warm dark" look.
Fix carried forward from 1: add the window-pane gobo to 2 so the key light has shape and falloff.

# Pass 2 world (rebuilt after crit 1)

- **Material:** a bare concrete workroom (Concrete034 floor and walls), a brass balance beam on a blackened iron post
  and plinth, and two kinds of iron: the classic skillet (grey, rough, heavy-walled, deep) and Ferro (thin-walled, satin
  black with a seasoned sheen). No food: the jury called it primitive, and it isn't the promise.
- **Key light:** cool morning daylight through a window, high left (`window` rig, gobo panes, warmth 0.15), so the
  brass beam catches one hard highlight and every object throws a long window-pane shadow on the concrete.
- **Palette family:** concrete greys (#8E8B86 → #D6D3CD), iron black, brass (#B8893A) as the one accent — the beam
  that carries the proof.
- **Type voice:** Alfa Slab One — the fat slab of foundry pattern letters and stamped shop scales. Type exists only
  *on the object*: "HALF THE WEIGHT" raised on the beam's face; the name as five cast letters dropped on the beam; the CTA
  raised on the plinth. Nothing laid over the picture.
- **Sound:** acoustic band at 120 bpm; foley of iron on brass trays, the beam's knife-edge creak, a ring when it
  settles level; a bar of silence at level.
- **Change per shot:** macro tray slam → wide off-axis → low looking up at the flung pan → high 3/4 as the second
  pan lands → wide low left as it swings → macro along the beam face → symmetrical front for the name.
