# 1 · The promise

**Promise (≤15 words, no name):** The sound that happens once is already kept — you only had to reach for it.

**Mode:** proof-leaning teaser. The world (a sunlit rehearsal room) is on screen from frame 0; the object enters at ~5 s as the *cause* of the change (lifted into the light), the name lands at the end.

Why this promise: a previous Tern film built on "nothing clips" (sound as landscape); its critique said the one truth only Tern owns — *it records the moment you lift it* — was captioned, never dramatized. This film is built on that truth alone.
