## What all the good ones do that ours usually don't
- They choose **one metaphor and make it physical**, then show it in very different vignettes. Ours repeat one layout.
- **The structure itself carries meaning** (a journey, a gestation, a wind-up), not a list of beats leading to a logo.
- **The sound comes from the subject** (footsteps, clockwork).
- **The logo is withheld or built from the world's own parts**, never a separate slide.
- **The light is one raking key on real texture.**

## The cliché of this category to avoid
"Prompt box → sparkles → video appears", a generated-AI montage, glowing nodes and lines, a terminal typing JSON. Also, for this world, a marching band "spelling the logo" *as the only point*. The second crit named exactly this ("the idea serves a logo reveal"), so the formations must mean something before the name arrives: the band has to perform a **picture that moves**, a film made of people.
