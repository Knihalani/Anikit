# Arctic sea ice, 1979–2026 (30 s explainer)

- `arctic-sea-ice.mp4`: final render (1920×1080, 30 fps, 30.0 s, generated orchestral/piano score)
- `contact-sheet.png`: one frame per second (0–29 s)
- `arctic.json`: Anikit Video spec, generated by `build_spec.py` from `N_09_extent_v4.0.csv`
- Render: `python3 build_spec.py && anikit-video render arctic.json -o arctic-sea-ice.mp4`

Data: NSIDC Sea Ice Index, Version 4 (G02135), Arctic September monthly average extent,
fetched from https://noaadata.apps.nsidc.org/NOAA/G02135/north/monthly/data/N_09_extent_v4.0.csv
(1979–2024 from NSIDC-0051, 2025–2026 from NSIDC-0803 near-real-time).

Derived figures (computed in `build_spec.py`): least-squares trend −0.74 million km²/decade
(−11.6 %/decade relative to the 1981–2010 mean, shown as −12 %); fitted 1979→2026 decline 3.48 million km²
(India: 3.29 million km²). Record low: 2012, 3.57 million km². Discs in shot 2 are area-proportional.

Note: the on-screen figures are the September *monthly average* extent, NSIDC's standard series for the
annual minimum; the single-day minimum is slightly lower each year.
