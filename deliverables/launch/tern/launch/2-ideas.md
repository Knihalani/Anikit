# 2 · Ideas

1. **The land is the recording.** An estuary at dawn whose far ridgeline *is* the waveform: every sound that happens (a drip, birds, wind, thunder) raises the land; the land plus its mirror in the water reads as a waveform. The loudest sound becomes a mountain that runs off the top of the frame — and the camera pulls back so its summit is never cut flat.
   - Literal test (delete "Tern" and "32-bit float"): "a dawn landscape that rises with every sound it hears, and nothing gets cut off" — survives.
   - Swap test: a generic recorder could borrow the look, but the two beats that carry it — *never flat-topped* and *the land wakes the instant you lift it* — are this product's own facts. Passes weakly → strengthened by making the lift the cause of the final wake.
2. **The one-take day.** One continuous camera move through a musician's day; every place it passes has one unrepeatable sound, and the move never cuts. Literal: survives. Swap: fails (any recorder, any phone) → generic. Rejected.
3. **Ink seismograph.** A pen on an endless paper strip traces sounds; thunder throws the pen past the paper edge — but the paper widens. Literal: it's the 32-bit float mechanism drawn as a picture → literal. Rejected.
4. (out there) **Sounds as birds.** A flock of terns made of sounds; each call is a bird; you lift your hand and the flock lands in it. Literal: the product's name drawn as a picture (tern = bird). Rejected.
5. **Frozen room.** Everything in a room freezes mid-air at the instant of a sound (glass mid-shatter) until the object is lifted. Strong but expensive 3D physics for 20 s; swap test weak. Rejected.

## Pick: 1 — the land is the recording
- **Device:** what visibly changes because the recorder exists — the world's sounds are kept as land, whole; nothing gets planed off.
- **Escalation:** a drip raises a twitch (1) → birdsong raises hills (2) → wind rolls a range across the valley (3) → thunder throws a mountain off the frame and the camera retreats to keep its peak whole (4) → silence, the land goes flat (stop) → the object is lifted into frame and the line wakes from it (payoff) → the land settles under the name (end).
- **Ending:** the line that has carried the film settles as the ridgeline under the name *Tern*, still breathing.
- **Why it won:** it escalates by scale (twitch → hills → mountain), it gives one rendered material and one light for the whole film, and its two key beats are the product's own facts, not captions.
