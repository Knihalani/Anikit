# 3. One world

- **Material:** cast iron (near-black, satin, slight imperfections) on a pale gritstone hearth / worktop (Yorkshire sandstone — Sheffield's own building stone). Nothing else: no props swap in.
- **Key light:** one low sun through a window (the `window` gobo panes), warm 3200–4500 K, hard-edged, raking. It is the clock: its azimuth sweeps to show time. Cool sky fill only. Arc: dawn amber → white noon → amber again at the end.
- **Palette family:** iron black, warm stone (#B9A588 → #D8C8AA), sun amber (#F2B66B) reserved for the device (the light, and the years written in it).
- **Type voice:** Besley (Clarendon-era 1840s serif) — the voice of Victorian Sheffield trade marks and foundry castings. It lives in the world two ways only: the years are *cast in light* on the stone (gobo text), and the name is *cast in iron* on the base of the pan (extruded text). No overlaid captions except the CTA, which is also written in light.
- **Sound world:** a slow acoustic/orchestral bed (low strings, felt piano) at a tempo that makes 20 s whole bars; foley from the subject: iron set on stone (a heavy clunk) at the start, a ticking that accelerates as time races, one hard stop, then the iron ring when the pan is turned and set down.
- **Anikit means:** scene3d, lathe-built pan + handle, ambientcg sandstone texture on the hearth, `window` rig / animated key light, gobo text for the years, extruded Besley for the cast mark; a light film grain in post.

## What changes shot to shot
Subject (whole pan / rim macro / handle shadow / years on the stone / base), scale (macro → wide → overhead), camera (low push, overhead topdown, side slide, rise over the base).

## Style frames (launch/frames/)
Three stills of the same key moment (the pan with "2058" written on the stone), varying the light: (A) a hard shaft of sun on the pan with the year as light through letters on a dark hearth; (B) the whole room lit, year as a shadow; (C) a dim night room, year as light. Compare: `frames/compare.png`, winner `frames/f0b.png`.
**Picked A:** it is the only one where the light itself is visibly the thing that tells time — one sun pool, one written year, black iron in between — and it reads as a poster with ~50% dark stone carrying texture rather than flat fill.
