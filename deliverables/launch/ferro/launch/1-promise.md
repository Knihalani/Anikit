# 1. The promise

**Promise (≤15 words, no name):** The pan you cook on tonight will still be cooking a hundred years from now.

**Mode:** proof. The real pan is on screen from frame 0; the film shows what happens around it, not a withheld reveal.

## Not in this film
- Pre-seasoned (implied by the black patina; not a beat, not a caption).
- Half the weight of a classic skillet (not a beat; at most the end card — left off to keep one claim).
- Small Sheffield foundry → only as the cast maker's mark on the base at the end ("SHEFFIELD"), which is where a foundry would really put it.
- Kickstarter → the call to action only.
