# 1 · The promise

**Every plant gets exactly the water it needs, exactly when it needs it.** (13 words, no name)

**Mode: proof.** The real product is visible by 5 s: the small sensor clipped into the pot reading the soil (≈4 s), then the app's instruction (when + how much) as the cause of the pour.

## Not in this film
- How the sensor measures (no probes diagram, no data charts).
- Per-plant profiles, species database, history, notifications settings — none of these are facts in the brief, so none are shown.
- No invented numbers (ml, % moisture, battery life, price, platforms). "How much" is shown as a fill line the water stops at, not a figure.
- End card at most: the name and one call to action.
