# 3 · One world: the darkroom tray

- **Material:** fibre photo paper (warm white, slight tooth) under a shallow sheet of developer in a black enamel tray. Every shot is in the tray, on paper, under liquid. The liquid is always alive: slosh, ripples, refraction of what is under it.
- **Key light:** one safelight, high and to the upper-left of frame, amber-red (#FF3A12 → #FF7A30 core), with inverse-square falloff across the tray and its reflection as a moving specular glint on the ripples. After the hard stop the safelight clicks off and the room light (a neutral-warm overhead, same position, white) comes on: an arc of the same light logic, not a swap.
- **Palette family:** under the safelight everything is monochrome in one ink: deep oxblood blacks → red → amber highlights. After the stop: the prints' true colours under white light, the tray still black, the paper still warm white. Accent reserved for the device: the moment an image starts to *move* (the beam of the lighthouse, the warm window in the glass shot).
- **Type voice:** Newsreader (news serif with optical sizes) — the voice of a caption printed on the border of a photograph; the agent's sentence is set as the print's caption, in italic, and it develops in the developer like the picture. The name is set in Newsreader display weight on its own sheet, developing like every print. No type is laid over the world: all of it is on paper, under liquid.
- **Sound world:** developer sloshing, paper sliding into liquid, the enlarger timer's tick (on the beat), the safelight switch click at the stop; music is a low cello + piano (acoustic) at 96 bpm, entering when the first picture starts to move.
- **Anikit means:** a custom GLSL filter for the tray (height-field liquid, refraction, specular glint of the key, falloff, safelight→room grade, grain); a develop filter inside each print (darks come up first, uneven development); print contents are real Anikit films: library ocean/sky/ink shaders + 2D paths (lighthouse), ray-traced `scene3d` (glass tumblers in window light), kinetic type, fluid.
- **Changes shot to shot:** subject (words / lighthouse / glass / a tray of many prints / twins / name), scale (macro on letters → full print → wide tray), camera (rotation and drift of the tray, push, pull, slide).
