# 3 · One world

- **Material:** paper on a desk. Bright white bond (sender), canary-yellow copy stock (receiver), all with real tooth (`paper` stock + `cutout` sheets with lifted shadows), on a dark charcoal felt blotter (canvas stock).
- **Key light:** a desk lamp from upper-left, warm (≈3200 K). Expressed as a directional falloff (warm-lit top-left, ~45% darker bottom-right corner, multiply), and cast shadows down-right from every lifted sheet. No radial glows anywhere.
- **Palette family:** felt charcoal #2A2926, bond white #F3EEE2, canary copy #F2D86A, writing ink #1B1A18, **accent reserved for the device: carbon blue #2D3FB5** (only ever the arrived mark and the name).
- **Type voice:** 
  - the writing is hand (`brush` ink strokes; "Homemade Apple" for words) — the world's own marks;
  - the name and the one CTA line are a *pressed impression* in Schibsted Grotesk (newspaper grotesque with ink-trap bite: reads as something printed/pressed, not a SaaS sans), in carbon blue, printed on the yellow copy, never on a backdrop.
- **Sound world:** felt-tipped piano + pizzicato and a soft brushed kit at 128 bpm; foley from the subject: pen scratch on paper, paper slide, sheet tear, a stamp-like thud on the landing.
- **Anikit means:** 2D graphic print — `paper`, `cutout`, `brush`/`sketch` strokes with draw-on, camera moves (zoom/pan), `sound: auto` + explicit cues.
- **Change per shot:** subject (pen mark → reveal → two sheets apart → field of sheets → blank sheet → name), scale (macro → desk → floor → macro), camera (push-in, tilt-lift, wide static, pull-out, locked, slow push).

## Style frames (launch/frames/frames.json → compare.png)
Same key moment (the payoff: top sheet torn away, the name is the impression on the copy), one decision varied: **type treatment**.
- A: name as a set grotesque impression, centred on the copy, torn strip at top. Clean but reads as a logo card on yellow.
- B: name as a *handwritten carbon impression* in carbon blue with an underline flourish, the torn white original still lifted at left. 
- C: giant cropped grotesque filling the frame. Strong poster, but "Rail / yard" split reads as the literal pun the skill warns against, and the hand is gone.

**Picked B:** it is the only one where the name is visibly *the same stroke that was sent* — the device itself resolves into the name, and the torn original in frame gives scale and a real cast shadow. CTA line goes printed (Schibsted Grotesk) on the copy, small, under it.
