# Substation: line-up announcement (c-event demo rebuild), 15 s, 9:16

**Idea:** the power station is the world. One continuous descent down a switchgear riser: an incomer panel (SUBSTATION, lit like sodium tubes), then one circuit per artist. On each beat, current runs down the copper busbar, the circuit closes with a real electric arc, and that arc (not a glow effect) is the light that reveals the name. Every circuit is its own poster built from a power-station object: copper contacts (ANCIENT METHODS), a flashover up an insulator stack (PAULA TEMPLE), letters full of plasma (DVS1), a cable bundle (HÉCTOR OAKS), a plasma field (RROSE), the danger plate (PHASE FATALE), one caged lamp in a dark hall (KIA), a row of lamps firing (IDA ENGBERG). The circuits speed up from every 3 beats to every 2; then the station powers up (shake, sub boom, hum opens up) and pulls back to the whole riser lit, and the lockup (TICKETS FRIDAY). The sound comes from the subject: a 49 Hz mains hum, relay clunks and arc crackles on every switch, under a 132 BPM techno kick and acid line.

**Files:** `final.mp4` (best judged pass), `contact.jpg`, `event.json` (spec, generated by `build.py`), `score.json` (by `score.py`), `concrete.png` (shader-made texture), `judge_results/`. `build_v3_lineup_poster.py` is the pass 3 variant (it ends on a full line-up poster); it scored lower and is kept for reference.
