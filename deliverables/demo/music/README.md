# Low Tide — Harbour Lights (demo j-music)

The brief: a 20 s 16:9 lyric video for the chorus of "Low Tide", music included.

**One device, escalated: the harbour lights write the lyric.**
- **Hook:** the quay lamps switch on along the water, one per piano eighth.
- **"Hold me at low tide":** the words ignite, lamp by lamp, on the melody notes, and are seen only in the rippling water.
- **"When the harbour lights are gone":** the tide is out, wide over the mud flats and the stranded boats. On "lights" the lamps flare with the celesta; on "gone" they fail one by one and stutter as they die. A last light is left burning.
- **"We were never meant to stay":** the words appear as cold lamp reflections, seen from above, and the tide surges back to break up "stay". Then the music stops for a half-beat breath.
- **"But we stayed on":** the light returns amber, every lamp becomes "on", and the camera pulls back.

The colour arc runs from blue hour, to violet night, to silver moonlight, to amber.

**Music** (`music.json`, timing in `score.json`):
- 84 bpm in D. A cello section sings the line, one note per syllable.
- Under it: Splendid grand piano, VSCO-2 string sections and contrabass, a Virtuosity kick and crash, and celesta for the lights.
- The harmony moves Bm–G–A, then to the borrowed G minor on "stay", and resolves to D on "stayed on".
- A synthesised sea drains with the tide and surges back.
- Every lyric word is a text layer timed to its note. Its opacity powers that word's lamps in `lamps.glsl`, which reads the lyric through a shader channel.
- Master: −14.0 LUFS, −2.2 dBFS true peak after AAC.

**Files:** `final.mp4`, `contact.png`, `spec.json` (generated by `python3 build.py`), `lamps.glsl`, `music.json`, `score.json` (`python3 score.py`), and `judge/*/result.json`.

Rendered with `ANIKIT_RENDER_CACHE=0` in about 5.6 min. `scripts/defects.py` reports `OK`.
