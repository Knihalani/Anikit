# Railyard — "Arrivals" (15 s, 16:9) — notes
1. Idea (concept.md): a split-flap departure board for money, where one row departs and arrives in the same second; Po-escape of the obvious "money flying fast" — no trains, no particles, the speed is a number.
2. Structure: story through an interface + reversal. Hook 0 s macro on flaps mid-clatter landing on DELAYED; pull back to a board of waiting money (FRI, +2 DAYS, HELD); 4 s the row clatters POST /V1/TRANSFERS.
3. Turn at 6 s (bar 4 downbeat): SENT 09:41:07 = ARRIVES 09:41:07; the dispatcher's light narrows to that row; status CREATED → PENDING → SETTLED, each with a WEBHOOK > TRANSFER.* row and a station chime.
4. Payoff 10 s: whole board clatters into RAILYARD + "ONE API CALL. INSTANT SETTLEMENT. / A WEBHOOK AT EVERY STATE CHANGE." held ~2.5 s; button: push in, DELAYS: NONE (rhymes with frame 0).
5. One material pushed: the flap. gen.py simulates every cell as a real split-flap (in-order alphabet, 34 ms per flap, cells stop at different times), with falling-flap shadows, hinge slits and pins — ~10k 2D layers.
6. Sound: 671 cued ticks/clicks generated from the same flap simulation (the clatter is the rhythm), chimes per webhook, a custom score at 120 bpm moving D minor (waiting) → D major (arrival): piano pulse, piano, strings, bass, glockenspiel, brushes.
7. World rules: enamel black-green, warm-white ink, amber only for waiting states, green only for settled/brand; flaps are the only type; locked telephoto camera, no whips.
8. Built with anikit-video 2D primitives only (no 3D/shaders): spec.json is generated by `python3 gen.py`; render `anikit-video render spec.json -o final.mp4 --crf 17` (~23 s).
9. Fix along the way: two tracks on one instrument collided and silenced the first 4 s — named the pulse track; final mix −13.9 LUFS integrated.
10. Style frames and the contact sheet are in frames/; amounts and event names on the board are illustrative, no invented customers or stats.
