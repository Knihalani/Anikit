# GAPS: NIGHT CHORUS (kinetic-typography title sequence)

What in the engine held this piece back, ranked by how much it cost the work. Each has the workaround I used.

| # | Gap | Kind | Impact | Workaround used |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Only 3 bundled typefaces.** A title sequence in this tradition is *made* of display type (12 families here: Anton, Monoton, Bebas Neue, UnifrakturMaguntia, Six Caps, Rubik Mono One, Bodoni Moda, Archivo Black, Instrument Sans…). | missing feature | Without a font library the piece is impossible; a cold agent would ship Inter. | Downloaded 23 OFL families from Google Fonts by hand (`deliverables/ceiling1/fonts/`) and listed them in top-level `fonts`. |
| 2 | **The key of the `fonts` map is ignored, and an unknown `font` silently falls back to Inter.** `"fonts": {"Bodoni Moda Black": "x.ttf"}` registers the file under its internal family (`Bodoni Moda`); `"font": "Bodoni Moda Black"` then renders in Inter with no warning from `check` or `preview`. | bug / bad default | Cost an iteration; a cold agent would never notice and ship the wrong face. | Read the name table with fontTools, used the internal family + `weight` + `*italic*`. **Fix:** register the key as an alias, and make `check` error on an unresolved family (with "did you mean"). |
| 3 | **No per-glyph material.** Giving each letter of a word its own shader treatment needs one full-frame shader layer per letter, each with its own text channel where the other letters are coloured `#FFFFFF00` (so the layout stays identical). The title shot is 11 shaders × ~100–200 ms = **~2.5 s/frame**; the 12.7 s piece took 4.5 min, 80% of it on the 4 s of title. | missing feature / slow path | Made the title the slowest thing in the piece; per-letter styling is a core title-design move. | The colour-run trick above. **Fix ideas:** a glyph-index channel (each glyph's index + local uv written into a second channel so one shader can switch treatment per letter), or `split: chars` children accepting `shader` fills. |
| 4 | **No glyph distance field.** Neon tubes, outlines, inner glows and extrusions all want an SDF of the text; I approximated them with mip-blurred alpha (`textureLod`) and 14-tap offset loops. Tubes thin out at sharp corners and the extrusion costs ~900 ms/frame before tuning. | missing feature | Visible on the neon N/R at 100% (tube width varies) and on extrusion cost. | Mip-blur thresholds and fewer taps. **Fix:** `"channels": [{"layers": [...], "sdf": true}]` (an EDT of the channel's alpha) would make neon, stroke-in-shader, bevels and extrusions exact and cheap. |
| 5 | **Default scene transition is a 0.5 s crossfade.** For cut-driven work (and BAR/TASTE both say "hard cut by default") every scene silently overlaps its neighbour and the video gets shorter than the sum of durations. My first test showed a second card fading in under the first. | bad default | Every beat-locked sequence starts misaligned until you find it. | `"transition": "cut"` at the top level. **Fix:** default to `cut`; crossfade is opt-in. |
| 6 | **`still --width` / thumbnails mis-render shader cards.** Shaders run at the reduced size with px-constant cells, so bulbs, halftone and LED screens alias into blobs in sheets and `--width 480` stills (the bulb card became unreadable). The `preview` sheet is misleading for any procedural-texture piece. | bug | I first "fixed" problems that were thumbnail artifacts. | Rendered every still full-size and downscaled with Lanczos. **Fix:** render previews at full size and downscale, or pass `iScale` so shaders can scale constants. |
| 7 | **No text-on-a-texture fill for native text.** Gradient fills work, but a text layer can't take a pattern/shader fill (stripes, halftone) without the composition-channel + shader detour. | missing feature | Every treatment needs a channel composition and GLSL. | `fxlayer()` helper in `build.py` wraps text into a channel and runs a treatment shader over it. |
| 8 | **Music beats detector bars are off** (`anikit-video beats score.wav` placed bar 1 at 1.41 s on a 128 BPM score whose downbeat is at 0.0 s). | bug (minor) | Would mislead anyone cutting to an external track. | Used the known grid (beat = 60/128). |
| 9 | **Reading-time and contrast warnings don't know about intentional strobe credits** (Enter-the-Void grammar: one name per beat at 0.47 s). `preview` reports errors on every card. | noise | 36 notes, all expected; the useful ones are buried. | Ignored; declared `"intent": {"pace": "frantic"}`. |

## Not engine limits but worth routing
- I can't audition audio from here; the score was judged from an RMS envelope (`ffmpeg` → numpy). A built-in `anikit-video music … --envelope` (loudness per 100 ms, onset list) would help any agent check that a silence is really silent and that hits land on cuts.
- Rendering: 258 s for 381 frames on 4 cores; 2D cards render at ~10 fps, title cards at ~0.4 fps.

## Added after the final render
- **Render cache granularity:** moving one label in one 0.47 s scene re-rendered 15 of 16 chunks (`render cache: reused 1/16 chunks`, 252 s). An edit to one shot should only re-render that shot's chunks.

## v2 (the street): new gaps found while rebuilding after the blind judge

The blind judge (`scripts/judge.py`, 3 trials) scored v1 at **3.25** against refs at 8.79 (FAIL): slide-deck structure, no world/image/depth/light, effects with no subject. v2 rebuilds it as a ray-traced night street where every credit is a real sign (`v2/`). Scores are recorded in `../README.md`.

| # | Gap | Kind | Impact | Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | **Shader layers inside a composition render blank when the composition is a 3D texture** (plain text compositions work). [3D/SHADER] | bug | Every animated sign treatment (bulb chase, moiré, flicker) was lost on the 3D signs. | Pre-rendered each v1 card with `still` to PNG and textured the signs with image compositions (static). |
| 11 | **Textured surfaces are lit only by the env and key light, not by local point/spot lights** (`print_light` in `trace.rs`). Brick facades lit by sodium lamps and sign spill show no pools of light, only a flat env tint. [3D] | missing feature | The judge's top complaint is "greybox / no motivated light on materials". Textures make the walls flat and evenly lit. | Baked a darkening gradient into the brick texture; kept local lights for untextured surfaces. |
| 12 | **No material library and no normal/roughness maps.** Brick, asphalt, weathered paint, neon-tube glass: everything is a flat colour or an albedo-only print. The judge calls the street "previs". [3D] | missing feature | The biggest remaining gap to the references. | Shader-generated brick and asphalt textures pre-rendered to PNG (`v2/tex/`). Albedo-only textures on the road killed the wet reflections, so the road stays untextured. |
| 13 | **Box sides take no texture** (only ±Z faces print), so a facade needs dozens of rotated panes over the box. [3D] | footgun | 220 extra objects. | Panes. |
| 14 | **The `smooth` camera spline overshoots between distant keys** (the title billboard ended up framed off-centre), and there is no tension control or per-key ease. [3D] | bug | Cost an iteration. | `"interp": "linear"` with hand-shaped whip and hold keys. |
