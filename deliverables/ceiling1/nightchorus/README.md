# NIGHT CHORUS: a kinetic-typography title sequence (12.7 s, 1920×1080)

**Idea (one sentence):** an original opening-title sequence for a fictional film set over one night in a city, where every credit is spoken by a different kind of city light (neon tube, bulb marquee, moiré, halftone poster, CRT chroma split, blackletter tattoo sign, extruded storefront letters, LED jumbotron, sodium stripes), and the title is the payoff: each letter of NIGHT CHORUS is one of those voices, singing together. Then the lights go out one by one.

**Files:** `nightchorus.mp4` (final), `nightchorus.json` (the spec; generated by `build.py` + `fx.py`), `score.json` (the score, authored in the music language and embedded in the spec), `contact.png` (one still per shot), `GAPS.md`.

**What makes them great (and the bar I judged against):** every card is a designed image on its own; type is huge and decisive; the treatment *means* something (the film's world or theme); the edit is musical (cuts land on the score, with a breath before the title); texture (grain, flicker, misregistration) makes it feel made rather than generated.

## Structure (128 BPM, F minor; beat = 0.469 s)

- Bar 1: the neon studio card flickers on (tube buzz in the score).
- Bars 2–4: one credit per beat, each in its own light/material and colour field; snap zooms, scrolls and settling chroma/halftone/extrusion animate inside each card.
- Bar 4, last two beats: four 8th-note strobe cards (ADA / ADA / KOVÁČ / KOVÁČ, inverting).
- Bar 5: kick, then a near-silent swell (reverse cymbal) while the title's letters flicker awake one at a time in the dark.
- Bar 5 beat 3: impact; NIGHT CHORUS slams in, every letter a different voice; slow push.
- Bar 7: the lights go out letter by letter on 16ths; black.

## Engine features used
Custom fonts (12 OFL families via top-level `fonts`); JIT GLSL shader layers with `alpha: true` reading text compositions as mipmapped channels (bulbs, moiré, halftone, LED, chroma, extrusion, neon); expressions for flicker; per-scene cameras (snap zoom, shake, push); rich text colour runs to isolate single letters in identical layouts; a score authored in the note/drum language (custom synths, per-bar clips, sfx), `followCuts: false`; post (grain, halation, bloom, vignette).
