# OCTAVA: a graphic-design-led brand film (10.8 s, 1920×1080)

**Idea (one sentence):** the identity of a (fictional) festival for new music is a grid of 8 rows (one octave), and the film *is* its score: every marimba note you hear lights the cell at (time, pitch). The ostinato phases against the bar the way Reich's music does, three canon voices stack when the camera pulls back, the dates slam in on the beat, and the melody finally spells the wordmark module by module. Picture and sound are generated from the same note list (`build.py`), so they cannot drift.

**Files:** `octava.mp4`, `octava.json` (spec, generated by `build.py`), `contact.jpg`, `GAPS.md`.

**What makes them great:** the system comes from the subject (music → grid → modules); every element sits on the grid; colour is few and saturated; type is either huge or small, never in between; nothing moves without the system moving it.

## Structure (120 BPM, D dorian; 8 grid rows = D E F G A B C D)
- Bars 1–2: the score scrolls past a lime playhead, edge to edge (8 rows × 135 px = 1080). Marimba alone, then vibraphone (vermilion, one 16th later: phasing), pizzicato bass.
- End of bar 2: the camera pulls back to 0.42× to reveal three canon voices stacked (glockenspiel in lime above, a fourth in ink below).
- Bar 3: vermilion ground; 14—29 / MAY / 2027 / BASEL in Archivo Expanded Black, one word per beat, alternating directions.
- Bars 4–5: chalk ground; the marimba plays two module-columns per 16th and the wordmark OCTAVA builds as it plays (row = pitch); info column; ~1.2 s silence; a vermilion module drops onto the grid on the last hit.
