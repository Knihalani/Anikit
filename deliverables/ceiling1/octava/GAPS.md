# GAPS: OCTAVA (graphic-design-led brand film)

Ranked by impact, with the workaround I used.

| # | Gap | Kind | Impact | Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No music↔picture binding for events.** The core of this piece (each note lights a cell) needs the visuals and the score generated from one list. The engine gives `@beatN` times and `level`/`beat` expressions, but no way to say "this layer appears when *this note* plays" or "emit a layer per note of track X". | missing feature | Without a Python generator this piece can't be authored; an agent writing JSON by hand would drift or give up. | `build.py` holds the note list and writes both the layers and the note-language strings. **Fix idea:** `"onNotes": {"track": "marimba", "layer": {...$pitch, $t...}}`, i.e. a repeater driven by score events (pitch, velocity, time), or `notes` exposed to `script`. |
| 2 | **Font family names are a guessing game.** `Archivo_wdth_wght_125_900.ttf` reports "Archivo Expanded Black" in name IDs 1 and 16, but the engine registers it as **"Archivo Expanded"**. The first two names silently fell back to Inter (the first previews had Inter where Archivo Expanded was intended). | bug / bad default | Two of three pieces hit this. | Trial and error with a test spec. **Fix:** register every name-table family (IDs 1, 16 and 21) plus the `fonts` key; error on unresolved families; `anikit-video info --fonts spec.json` to list what's registered. |
| 3 | **Static contrast check ignores shape layers under text.** White labels on a full-frame cobalt rect are reported as "contrast 1.1:1" (measured against the spec's chalk `background`). 9 false errors. | bug | Buries real problems; trains agents to ignore errors. | Ignored (the render-contrast check is correct). **Fix:** take the topmost opaque shape under the text box into account, or drop the static check when a full-frame rect exists. |
| 4 | **Track-level `transpose` breaks a track that has `clips`.** `{"inst": "glockenspiel", "clips": [...], "transpose": 12}` errors with "a clip needs notes…" because clip fields are flattened onto the track. | bug (confusing error) | Cost an iteration. | Moved `transpose` into the clip. **Fix:** treat clip-level keys on a track that has `clips` as defaults for every clip, or say so in the error. |
| 5 | **`from` keyframes hold before their `at`.** A camera `{"from": {"zoom": 1.06}, "at": 6}` silently overrides the zoom for 0–6 s. It's documented, but surprising in a multi-move camera list. | bad default / footgun | Would have broken bars 1–3. | Chained `to` keys only. **Fix:** warn when a `from` with `at > 0` overlaps an earlier tween of the same property. |
| 6 | **No grid/layout primitive for design systems.** Columns, rows, a module size and snapping (`"grid": {"cols": 16, "rows": 8, "module": 135}` → `"cell": [c, r]`) would make Swiss-style work far faster to author than raw px. | missing feature | Everything is computed in Python. | `build.py`. |
| 7 | **The pace critique punishes deliberate holds in a brand film** (36% static seconds when the poster lockup holds). Switching intent to `steady` was the honest fix, but a "hold" marker for the final lockup would help. | noise | Minor. | `"intent": {"pace": "steady"}` plus a slow settle (zoom 0.9 → 1.0) during the lockup. |

Render cost: 10.8 s at 1080p30 in 10–20 s (16–33 fps), 274 layers.
