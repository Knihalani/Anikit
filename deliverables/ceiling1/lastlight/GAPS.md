# GAPS: LAST LIGHT (cinematic 3D product film)

Ranked by how much they cost the work, with the workaround I used. Owners in brackets are guesses.

| # | Gap | Kind | Impact | Workaround |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Textures only print on faces whose local normal is ±Z** (`scene3d/trace.rs` `print()`: `front = s.on.z > 0.5`). Cylinders have no UVs at all (`geom::cylinder`). A record, a platter top, a tabletop, a label (anything lying flat) silently gets no texture, with no warning. [3D] | bug / missing feature | The record is the hero; this ate ~6 iterations. | Built the record as a `surface` in the XY plane (x = r cos u, y = r sin u), rotated −90° about X, with a **polar-unwrapped** composition (x = angle, y = radius). **Fix:** use the UV the mesh has, regardless of face orientation (`face` meaning front/back by normal sign along the mesh's own facing axis), give cylinders/lathes cap UVs (planar) and side UVs (u = angle, v = height), and warn when a texture can't map. |
| 2 | **Volumetric shafts are noisy and miss thin occluders.** At `standard` quality the haze is visibly blotchy (8 march steps at half resolution); `high` (32 steps) halves the grain but it stays visible, and venetian-blind slats (thin boxes) never resolve into stripes in the beam, only as one dark wedge. There is no temporal accumulation or denoise for the volume. [3D] | quality limit | The "light through blinds" image I wanted (the reference look) was impossible; I kept a single clean shaft. Haze grain reads as film grain in motion, but stills show it. | `quality: high`, narrow spot (7°), low density (0.0035), and dropped the stripes as a look. **Fix:** blue-noise jitter plus temporal reprojection or a bilateral blur of the volume buffer; shadow-map lookups for local lights in the march, so thin occluders cut stripes. |
| 3 | **`camera: false` objects don't cast volumetric shadows.** Off-camera flags/blinds (the standard film-lighting move: a cutter outside the frame shaping the light) only shade surfaces, not the haze. [3D] | bug | Forced the blinds into frame or out of the design. | Moved them outside the frustum near the sun with `camera` left on. |
| 4 | **`exposure` is a linear multiplier, and `exposure: 0` gives a black frame with no warning.** Anyone thinking in EV ("0 = neutral") gets black. [3D] | bad default / footgun | First test frame was black. | `exposure: 1`. **Fix:** accept `"exposure": "+1ev"` or document "multiplier (1 = neutral)", and warn on 0. |
| 5 | **The `interior` env auto-exposes on the backdrop colour.** With a dark custom `color` the exposure multiplier explodes and the frame goes white; there's no opt-out (`auto_exp` is applied unconditionally in `scene3d/mod.rs`). [3D] | bug | Second test frame was white; abandoned `interior`. | `env: night` plus my own sun spot and a cool `dir` fill. **Fix:** an `"autoExposure": false`, or only auto-expose when no `exposure` is given. |
| 6 | **The engine assumes a ~5 m set.** Fog density is per metre and light falloff is physical, so a real-scale turntable (0.47 m) gets 10× too much fog and the spot's falloff is wrong. Nothing says so. [3D] | footgun | Two iterations of white-out. | Built everything at ×10 scale. **Fix:** a `"units"` / `"scale"` key, or doc "build at ~5 m scale". |
| 7 | **Z-fighting between coplanar surfaces** shows as dark radial spokes (the textured disc sat exactly on the record cylinder's top). No epsilon or warning. [3D] | quality | Looked like a shading bug. | Lifted the disc 0.005 units. |
| 8 | **No object parenting / rigs.** A tonearm is a pivot with children (tube, counterweight, headshell, cartridge, stylus). Every part had to carry its own rotation-about-pivot expression (`pivot + R(a)·offset`), and the tube's `path` is a piecewise `mix()` expression of `s` and `t`. [3D] | missing feature | ~40 lines of expression plumbing; easy to break. | `arm_xz()` helper in `film.py`. **Fix:** `"group": {"pivot": [...], "rot": "...", "objects": [...]}` in scene3d. |
| 9 | **No anisotropic / groove specular.** Vinyl's signature look is the radial sheen of the grooves; the material model is isotropic, so the record reads as matte black from most angles and the fine groove lines (1.4 px in a 4096 texture) average out at distance. [3D] | missing feature | The macro groove shot I planned (light raking across grooves) didn't read; replaced with a top-down shot. | Top-down framing; label carries the read. **Fix:** an `anisotropy` + `anisoDir: "radial"` material key. |
| 10 | **`music fetch salamander` (the documented syntax) fails** with "unexpected argument"; only `music fetch --pack salamander` works. [MUSIC] | bug | Minor. | `--pack`. |
| 11 | **No vinyl crackle / room tone / hiss in the sfx list.** [MUSIC] | missing sound | The needle-drop moment depends on it. | Built crackle from a noise synth (band-pass, 0.5 ms attack, 6 ms decay) on random 64th-note ghosts, plus a low-passed noise hiss. |
| 12 | **`blend: over` particles are lit only by the env key, not by local spot/point lights.** Dust motes in a sunbeam from a spot render black, so they're invisible in a night env. Additive particles glow everywhere, including in the dark half of the room. [3D/PARTICLES] | missing feature | Dust in the beam is *the* detail of this look. | Additive motes with a per-particle `opacity` expression that computes the distance from the beam axis (cone test in x, y, z) and fades them outside the shaft. **Fix:** shade `over` particles with local lights (with the spot cone and shadow), so physically placed dust just works. |
| 13 | **Cost:** 240 frames of high-quality ray tracing with volumetrics took **561 s (2.3 s/frame, 4 cores)**; standard quality is ~2.4 s per still in the loop. Iteration on lighting is slow compared with the 2D pieces. [PERF] | slow path | ~25 stills to find the look. | Iterated on single full-size stills; one final render. |

## After the blind judge (3.5 vs refs 7.71, FAIL)
The judge's main complaints were the "tasteful AI" end card (tracked italic serif over a small-caps tagline), one god-ray look repeated across frames, primitive geometry, and an "orrery" name the film never uses. Changes: an establishing opener into the sun; the end card replaced by **the last light projecting the wordmark** (a gobo beam through 3D text casting ORRERY in shadow letters on the wall); no 2D text at all.
