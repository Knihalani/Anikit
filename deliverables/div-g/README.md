# Offset 2027 — keynote title sequence (15 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps)

Concept: a Lisbon azulejo is itself a design system. One tile module draws itself on (01),
one rotation rule repeats it into circles (02), the rows offset on the beat and the rule turns
into a meander (03), then the wall resolves into a system: tiles flip to spell OFFSET (04),
and a strip of cobalt tiles carries the date and place.

- `offset2027.json` — Anikit Video spec (layers, tags, type, acoustic score at 96 bpm in E minor)
- `tiles.glsl` — the tile field: motif, rules, row offsets, tile flips, glaze and light
- `offset2027.mp4` — final render
- `contact-sheet.png` — one frame per second (0–14 s)

Re-render: `anikit-video render offset2027.json -o offset2027.mp4`
