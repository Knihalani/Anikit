## Proof object and honest labels
- **The proof object** is the sound report itself: every claim on the page is one of the brief's real facts (lift-to-record, 32-bit float, 30 h battery, four mics). No numbers, quotes, reviews or customers are invented. The "old one" is unnamed, so no competitor is named or disparaged.
