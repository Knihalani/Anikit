# 2 · Ideas (≥ 4, different families), then the pick

## A. "The night shift" — structural device: one night, compressed (time is the set)
The film is one night, 23:58 → 07:00, told by a huge clock that *is* the set and a ground whose light is the hour (ink night → first light). Email arrives all night as real inbox rows; Relay reads each one (a quiet read-mark sweeps the row; a one-line gist and a draft appear). At ~03:00 the night peaks in a reply-all storm (`Re: Re: Re: Fwd:` stacking past the frame). 06:59: silence. 07:00: everything folds into one page, "Your morning · 2 min". Then the payoff: Send, Send, Send — each on a beat, each card leaving — until the inbox is empty and the clock reads 07:02.
- Insider details: the 11:58 p.m. "small thing before tomorrow" email; reply-all storm; "Re: Re: Fwd:"; "Just circling back"; "Does Thursday work?"; inbox timestamps like 2:14 AM.
- Reference moves: v0's one-shot input→output; Raycast macro UI; Bass/Vertigo one device that escalates; Daylight "sell what it removes".
- Gates: **literal** — delete "Relay" and "AI agent": a night's worth of messages handled while someone sleeps, ending in a morning where you just say yes. Survives. **Swap** — "Superhuman" or "Gmail summaries" on it fails: neither works overnight and hands back drafted replies; the 07:00 handover is Relay's specific promise. **Renderable** — UI, type and a graphic sky gradient; no bodies, no bedroom. **Fresh** — prior films: darkroom tray, iron/stone, illustrated windowsill, paper on a desk, moorland shader, turf field. None is UI-and-clock. **Proof inside the moment** — without the product, nothing folds at 07:00 and there are no drafts to send.

## B. "The morning edition" — analogous craft: the newspaper night desk
Overnight the night desk turns the wire into a front page; at dawn it's on the step. The brief as a broadsheet built from your inbox. — **Rejected:** fresh test (railyard was paper on a desk) and banned look risk (paper + editorial serif). Swap test weak (any "digest" product).

## C. "The prompter" — out-there: the theatre prompt book
Your day as a script; your lines already written in the margin by the prompter; you just say them. Email threads typeset as a play: "PRIYA: Does Thursday work? / YOU: Thursday 2pm is perfect." Amusing. — **Rejected:** loses the overnight truth (the prompter works live, not overnight), and the screenplay costume hides the real UI, so the proof is weaker.

## D. "Mise en place" — the night prep kitchen
Prep cooks work overnight so service is just assembly. — **Rejected:** renderable test (food, kitchens), and literal-adjacent.

## E. "The baton" — relay-race handoff
— **Rejected:** literal test (the product's name drawn as a picture).

## Pick: A, "The night shift"
- **Device:** a night clock that is the set; the hour drives the light, the volume of email and the music.
- **Shareable moment:** at 07:00 the whole night folds into one page and you just tap Send.
- **Escalation (6 beats):** one email at 23:58 → a few, each read and drafted as it lands → 03:00 reply-all storm floods the frame → 06:59 silence (hard stop) → 07:00 fold into the brief → Send ×3 on the beat, inbox empties → name.
- **Ending made of the world:** the clock reads 07:02, the inbox is empty; the last row in the empty inbox is Relay's own sign-off: "Relay — read overnight, ready by morning."
- **Why it beat the others:** it's the only one where the device *is* the unownable truth (the work happens while you sleep), it escalates naturally (night builds, morning releases), and it keeps the real output (drafts, Send) on screen.
