# 1 · Promise, truth, moment

- **Brief facts:** Relay is an AI agent for your inbox. Overnight it reads every email. In the morning it leaves a two-minute brief with replies already drafted. You approve and send instead of writing.
- **Unownable truth:** the work happens *while you are asleep*, and what you wake to is not a summary but finished replies. Inbox tools (filters, AI summaries, smart compose) all still need you awake and writing. Relay's claim is the *handover*: a night of email becomes one morning page where the only verb left for you is "send".
- **Promise (≤ 15 words, no name):** Wake up to an inbox that's already been read and answered — you just approve.
- **Proof or teaser:** proof. The product's output (the brief, the drafted reply, the send) is on screen by 2 s and is the subject of every beat.
- **Shareable moment (≤ 15 words):** At 7:00 a whole night of email folds into one page, and you just tap Send.
- **Target emotion:** amusement then relief (the 3 a.m. reply-all storm everyone knows, then it simply goes away). Not contempt for email, which people rely on.
- **Send test:** a founder / manager DMs it to their co-founder or EA with "I need this" — it makes the sender look like they've found the fix to a shared pain first. Also the "reply-all storm at 3am" bit is a recognisable joke people forward.
- **Hostile retell:** "Another AI email summariser, with a clock on it." Not damning, but the risk is genericness — so the film must make the *drafted replies + approve/send* the payoff, not the summary, and the night (the time it does the work) the device.
- **Not in this film:** no model names, no integrations list (Gmail/Outlook not stated in the brief), no pricing, no counts of emails processed or time saved beyond the brief's "two-minute brief", no security claims, no team/enterprise features.
