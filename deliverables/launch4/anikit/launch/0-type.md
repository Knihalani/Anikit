**Type: Idea film carrying proof.** Anikit Video makes media, so the film *is* its own proof: every frame and every note of it is one JSON spec rendered by `anikit-video` (spec.json ships beside final.mp4). The idea is told through a graphic system (a pyrotechnic firing script and its shell diagrams), and in the turn the script is revealed to be the real spec lines that draw the show.
