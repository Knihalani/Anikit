# 1 · Promise, truth, moment

- **Unownable truth:** the same spec always gives the same film, byte for byte, picture *and* sound, rendered on the agent's own machine. Generative video models can't claim either (they sample; they need a GPU farm).
- **Promise (≤15 words, no name):** Your agent writes one page; a finished film with sound comes back, identical every time.
- **Shareable moment (≤15 words):** A typed cue list fires a whole fireworks show on the beat, then fires it again, identically.
- **Target emotion:** awe (precision at scale), with a small grin at the reveal that the cue list was JSON.
- **Send test:** an agent developer DMs it to the designer they work with: "our agent could make the launch film itself, and it's reproducible like a build." Makes the sender look first-to-know.
- **Hostile retell:** "a screensaver fireworks show with a JSON punchline." Not damning if the bursts are clearly *designed* (print/diagram, not simulated night sky) and the punchline is true: the cue list really is the spec that drew them.
- **Not in this film:** 3D/ray tracing, shaders, physics, particle clouds, kinetic-type feature list, render speed numbers, Python/TS packages, MCP, analyze/critique tools. (The rendering speed appears only as an honest line in the post.)
