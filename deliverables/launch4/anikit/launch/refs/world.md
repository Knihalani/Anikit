# The subject's world

Two worlds meet: AI-agent developers, and the pyromusical show designer (the analogous craft).

## Agent developers / designers (sure)
- They live in terminals, JSON tool calls, eval logs, diffs, CI. Value: reproducibility ("same input, same output"), a file you can diff, review and commit; no GPU queue.
- What they hate: non-determinism ("it worked on my run"), opaque cloud renders, watermarks, waiting.

## Pyromusical firing (the craft; mostly sure, from general knowledge, no web search done)
- A show is designed on a computer (Finale 3D, Pyrodigital/FireOne "Show Director"), choreographed to the music, then exported as a **firing script**: one row per cue with **event time** to 1/100 s or SMPTE frames, **module and pin** (the cue address), **position** on the barge/rack line, **calibre** (2", 3", 5" shells), and an **effect description** ("3in red peony", "5in gold willow", "brocade crown", "crossette", "salute").
- **Prefire / lift time:** the computer fires a shell *before* the music beat by its lift time so that it **breaks** exactly on the beat. Insider sentence: "the script fires early so the sky lands on time."
- The firing system then fires the show the same way every night; the operator's job is the arm key and the "hold". Countdown: "Show armed." Cues fire on SMPTE timecode locked to the soundtrack.
- **Shell anatomy / Japanese hanabi catalogues (warimono):** round shells pack **stars** in concentric shells; a **peony** is one ring of stars, a **shin-iri** (pistil) peony has rings within rings, a **chrysanthemum** leaves tails, a **willow/kamuro** droops long gold strands, a **ring** shell is a flat circle, a **crossette** splits into crosses. Catalogue illustrations are flat drawn dot rings on a plain ground: a graphic tradition, not a photo.
- **Daytime fireworks** (Cai Guo-Qiang, *Black Ceremony*, Doha 2011; *Elegy*): coloured smoke in pale daylight sky. The reference for a bright ground.
- Sound: the lift "thump" (mortar), the break "boom", crackle, the silence before the finale; the **finale** is a chain of hundreds of cues inside seconds.
