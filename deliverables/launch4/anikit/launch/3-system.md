# 3 · Visual system

**Derivation line:** *same spec → same film, to the frame* → **every mark in the sky is caused by one timed row of the page, and lands exactly on its time and beat.** Nothing moves unless a row fired it.

- **Subject per shot:** the firing script (the page, real spec text) and the shells it draws, as hanabi-catalogue figures. No simulated sky, no night, no smoke sim, no people, no city, no barge.
- **Material world:** flat print ink on a daylight sky. One ground: a cool pale sky (`#D3E1E9` top → `#E6EDEF` horizon), grain over everything. Light logic: daylight, soft top-to-horizon gradient; at the hold the sky dims a step (dusk) and the finale is on that dimmer sky (an arc, not a swap).
- **Palette:** sky ground; ink navy `#14213D` for all type; three shell inks: vermilion `#E8432A`, marigold `#E9A23B` (the brand's warm accent family, `--accent #ffb27a`), cobalt `#2346B8`. Vermilion is the accent (the active cue, the name's dot).
- **Type:** IBM Plex Mono (the firing script: machine text, cue times, JSON) + Archivo (claims and name; condensed `wdth 75`, `wght 800`). Sizes at 1080p: 30 px script, 64 px macro script, 150 px claim / name. Grid: 12 columns, 6% margins; the script lives in columns 1–5, the sky in 6–12; macro shots break the grid by cropping, never by re-centring.
- **Type inside the scene:** the cue rows are the world's document; claims are set as rows of the script (the claim line *is* a cue: `00:09.00  —  "Same page. Same show."`).
- **Motion vocabulary:** easings `outExpo` (breaks, arrivals), `inOutCubic` (camera / scroll moves), `inQuad` (fall, fade). Durations 0.25 / 0.6 / 1.6 s. Stars spread on `1-exp(-6.5t)` and fall on `30t²`: one physics rule for every shell.
- **Causes:** the only movers are the script cursor (time), the lift trails (prefire), the stars (break) and the camera when it moves to a row. No float, no drift, no glow, no particles haze.
- **Texture:** fine grain on the whole film. No glow, no bloom.
- **Depth:** layering only (script plane over sky; macro crops via camera zoom). No perspective.
- **CG blacklist:** count 0 (no 3D at all). **Banned looks:** none of indigo-violet, cream+terracotta, near-black+acid, glowing serif, chrome, tasteful-AI kit, neural/particle nets. Fireworks on black is explicitly avoided.
- **Per shot changes:** subject (row ↔ shell ↔ two nights ↔ name), scale (macro row ↔ full sky), camera (static / push to a row / split).
- **Sound:** orchestral (strings, brass, timpani) at 120 bpm, the pyromusical genre; booms and thumps from the sfx set on the breaks; one hard stop before the finale.
