# 2 · Ideas

Prior Anikit films: player-piano roll (amber light through paper), darkroom tray (paper prints), marching band (CG field, rejected as artificial). This film must leave paper-on-dark and must not simulate a real scene.

1. **The firing script (picked).** A pyromusical cue list, one row per shell with its time to 1/100 s, fires a whole show on the music, drawn as hanabi-catalogue figures in a daylight sky. It fires again on another night: identical, burst for burst. At the finale the cue list is revealed as JSON lines, the spec that drew this film; the finale's last chain writes the name.
   - Insider: firing script, prefire/lift time ("fires early so the sky lands on time"), module/pin, calibre, shin-iri pistil, willow, the hold before the finale.
   - Reference move: Bass/Whitney rule-driven figures on flat colour; hanabi catalogue drawing.
   - Literal test: delete the name and "JSON→MP4": "a typed list of times fires a fireworks show on the music, the same show every night." Survives: it's a world.
   - Swap test: Sora/Runway/Veo can't claim "the same show every night"; a code-to-chart library has no music or show. Fails for competitors → passes.
   - Renderable: drawn dot rings and strokes on a flat ground; no simulated sky, no people.
   - Proof inside the moment: the rows really are spec entries; delete the product and the reveal (the list was the film's own source) makes no sense.
2. **The bell-ringer's method.** Change-ringing: a method (a page of numbers) rung by six bells, every peal identical. Insider: "Plain Bob Minor", rounds, "that's all". Rejected: beautiful but slow and arcane; escalation is only more numbers; sound is the whole idea, picture weak.
3. **The knitting chart.** A grid chart → a jacquard pattern, stitch for stitch. Rejected: fails *picture and sound*; close to the piano-roll punched-paper family.
4. **(Out there) The tide table.** A printed table of times and heights, and the sea obeys it to the minute. Rejected: water is on the CG blacklist; determinism of nature isn't a made thing.

## Pick: 1 · The firing script
- **Device:** a page of timed cues fires a whole show of picture and sound, and it is the same show every time.
- **Escalation:** (1) one cue row, one ring breaks on its exact timecode → (2) rows fire in sequence, each a different shell, the sound locked to the breaks → (3) a chain: dozens of cues, the sky fills → (4) the seam: Tuesday's show and Saturday's show side by side, identical; overlaid, they are one → (5) hold: the music stops, cursor on `FINALE` → (6) the finale chain breaks and its stars settle into the name → (7) the cue list flips to its true form, JSON, beside the name and URL.
- **Shareable moment:** the cue list fires the show on the beat, then the two nights match exactly.
- **Ending:** the finale's stars are the name (the device writes it); the script reveals itself as the spec.
- **Why it won:** a fresh daylight graphic world, a sound world native to the subject (thump, break, crackle, orchestra), and determinism shown as a picture (two nights, one sky) with the product's real input as the punchline.
- Rejects kept: change-ringing, tide table.
