# Directed: *Halftime*

A worked example of the skill's directing method (`skills/anikit-video/SKILL.md`): a raw spec with no template.

**Logline:** for people drowning in pings, Halftime makes the world quiet for 25 minutes.
**Feeling:** relief, a held breath.
**Look:** `noir`. Silence reads as darkness and hard light, and the serif gives it gravity.
