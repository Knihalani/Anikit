# Shape: beats, timing, ending (step 5)

## Length and shots

- **Length:** per platform (`modes.md` table). X hero 30–60 s; Reels and TikTok 15–34 s; teaser 6–10 s.
- **Shot count:** 5–9 authored shots for 15–30 s. Every shot changes subject, scale or camera.
- **Each beat answers:** "What belief gets weaker if this beat is cut?" If the answer is nothing, cut the beat.

## Music first

- Generate or choose the track before animating (`anikit-video docs music`, `docs rhythm`). Set the bpm so the film is a whole number of bars: `bars = length × bpm / 240`.
- Cut on completed ideas at bar lines (`"@barN"`). Put the turn or payoff on a downbeat with a `hit`.
- Put **one hard stop** before the payoff: silence or a filtered drop of ½–1 bar.
- End on an instrumental resolve (`"end": "ring"`), not a fade mid-phrase.
- Let the music drive picture where it fits: `onNotes`, and `beat`/`level` in expressions.

## The beat sheet (`4-beats.md`)

| bar | shot (subject · scale · camera) | what changes | device level | sound |

The device shows in every beat and escalates. For example: a hint (1) → the change happens once (2) → it spreads (3) → at scale (4) → stop (5) → payoff (6) → the name made of the world (7).

## Type timing

- A card holds ≥ 0.3 s × words + 0.5 s after it settles. A single fast word holds ≥ 5–7 frames.
- One claim line on screen at a time.
- At least one word acts out its meaning.

## First and last frames

- **Frame 0 is a poster** already moving by frame 2: the product, its output or the anomaly. Never black, a fade, a logo or a title card. In proof films the product is *changing* by 1.5 s (`modes.md`).
- **The shareable moment** lands by 3 s in cuts ≤ 15 s; in longer cuts a first version lands by 3 s and the full payoff by 40% (`viral.md`).
- **The name** lands in the last 5–10% through an action of the film's world, at 15–30% of frame width, with the **real URL or handle**.
- **No static end card longer than 1.5–2 s.** Vertical cuts loop: the last 0.5 s matches the first frame.
