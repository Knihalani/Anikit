# Film types (step 0)

Pick one. Each has its own craft rules on top of the shared method. Sources: `docs/video/craft/launch-ai-viral.md` (40+ AI and software launches), `launch-visual-craft.md` (studio systems), `launch-research.md` (14 launch films).

## Proof film: the product working (default for software, AI and apps)

The format that spreads most for AI products: the real product, one magic moment, shown so the viewer believes it. Examples: GPT-4o's live voice demo, Claude Code's terminal films, Cursor's demos, v0 and Bolt's prompt-to-app reveals, Linear's product films, Arc's demos.

- **Real UI and output only:** supplied screenshots, components, or the product's real output. Every string is plausible domain content: real file names, real task names, no lorem, no "Feature 1". If the brief gives no UI, build it from the product's real behaviour, keep it modest, and say it's illustrative in the post.
- **Product moving by 2 s:** at t = 1.5 s the UI or output is *changing*. No title card, logo or empty frame first.
- **The first sentence on screen** is a claim or a problem in ≤ 10 words, not "Introducing X".
- **One magic shot:** input and result in one continuous shot with no cut, the result held readable for ≥ 2 s. This is the moment people share.
- **Macro UI:** at least half the UI shots crop to one component at ≥ 2× native size. Never show a whole app window below 60% of frame width. Return to context at most once per beat.
- **Cursor choreography** (when there's a cursor): one cursor on eased arcs. The camera starts moving toward a click ≥ 6 frames before it. The cursor settles ≥ 0.25 s before clicking, and the UI responds within 4–6 frames.
- **Honest time:** any compressed wait carries "sped up" or "2×". Chart bars are computed from their numbers.
- **Pace:** in the demo section the median state change is ≤ 2 s; the magic shot is the one exception.
- **Depth:** build it by layering flat planes (overlap, scale, parallax). Use a perspective camera only on real 3D objects. Never orbit a flat screen, never float devices in space.

## Idea film: a brand film in type, diagram or graphic system

For launches whose truth is a feeling or a principle, and for products with no UI to show. Examples: OpenAI's brand films (dots and type), Pentagram's identity films, Linear's brand pieces, Apple's "Don't Blink" (type at speed).

- **Derivation line:** the whole visual system comes from one product behaviour → visual rule. For example: "deterministic → every frame is plotted on one grid and replays identically", or "instant settlement → nothing in frame ever waits". Every scene cites it.
- **Media that work:**
  - kinetic type with a real typographic voice;
  - diagrams, maps and charts, drawn not simulated;
  - graphic and print systems (halftone, riso, grid choreography);
  - abstract shader or simulation worlds that never pretend to be a real place.
- **An analogy from a real craft** (a drill chart, a score, a cue sheet) is shown as that craft's *document or diagram*, never as a CG simulation of the people doing it.
- **Type rules:**
  - ≤ 2 families (one may be mono), ≤ 3 sizes per frame, a strict column grid, outer margins ≥ 5% of the width;
  - at least one word acts out its meaning;
  - read time is ≥ 0.3 s × words + 0.5 s.

## Object teaser: a physical product

For hardware and objects. Examples: Nothing, Apple product films, Teenage Engineering, Devialet, Bang & Olufsen.

- **The product is the only CG subject.** Use its real geometry, materials and finishes (scene3d PBR materials, machined finishes, decals). The surroundings are abstract forms driven by a product parameter, or a seamless studio, never a simulated real place.
- **Light:**
  - a motivated key with falloff;
  - one strip-light sweep that reveals form;
  - macro rack focus onto a real detail (a texture, an engraving, a port, a mechanism).
- **Show the mechanism:** reveal how it works (a part moving, a material behaving), not the product rotating on a turntable.
- **CG blacklist:** humans, animals, plants, terrain, water, food, fog banks, crowds, chrome type on a sweep, physics props as metaphor (dominoes, tiles, balls, cloth). The count must be 0.

## Platform cuts (step 9)

| Platform | Aspect | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| X / LinkedIn hero | 16:9 | 30–60 s (longer works if it holds) | native upload |
| Reels / TikTok | 9:16 | 15–34 s | loops; key text inside the centre safe area (clear of top 10% and bottom 20%) |
| YouTube Shorts | 9:16 | 15–45 s | |
| Teaser | any | 6–10 s | |

Compose each cut as its own layout, never a crop. The moment must read in every cut.
